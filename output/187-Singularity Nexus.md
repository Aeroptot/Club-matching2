## 社团介绍

Singularity Nexus — where Mathematics, Physics, Algorithms, and Strategy converge. Basic Information: Singularity Nexus is a club dedicated to logical thinking and advanced problem-solving across Mathematics, Physics, Algorithms, and Game Theory. Weekly sessions include teaching core concepts and problem-solving techniques, followed by discussions of challenging problems designed by club leaders. Each year, we create original problems for competitions. Purpose: We bring together students who are passionate about logical thinking and interdisciplinary problem-solving. The club provides a platform for members to exchange ideas, find like-minded peers, and support each other’s learning. Through peer teaching and collaborative discussion, we aim to strengthen SCIE students’ competitiveness and enrich campus life.
