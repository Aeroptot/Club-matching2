## 社团介绍

Our ECA is an Equestrian club called We & Horse. When talking about equestrian, many students simply say it is “horse riding”. However, people neglect that relationship between rider and horses is more significant.before we ride horses, we need to learn how to respect for horses, atthe same time we would receive therapeutic connection from them, so the target of the ECA is letting more people to have not only deeper understanding of equestrian but knowledge of horses, so that helps people to build a healthy relationship with horses and nature.
