## 社团介绍

Yearbook is a student-led ECA dedicated to documenting and celebrating the SCIE community through creative storytelling, photography, design, and collaboration. The ECA works to capture memorable moments from academic events, student activities, sports, ECAs, and everyday campus life, creating a meaningful record of each school year. Members will develop skills in content creation, visual design, communication, project management, and teamwork while contributing to a shared project that represents the diverse voices and experiences of SCIE students. Through collecting stories, designing layouts, and coordinating with different departments and student groups, Yearbook provides students with opportunities to strengthen their creativity and leadership skills while preserving the school’s collective memories.
