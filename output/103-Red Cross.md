## 社团介绍

SCIE Red Cross, founded in 2010, is one of the oldest and largest societies in Shenzhen College of International Education. It is an outstanding social ECA certified by international organizations, dedicated to promoting humanitarian spirit of borderless care. Under the guidance of Shenzhen Red Cross headquarters and school medical teachers, we integrate first aid training, charity volunteer activities and mental health promotion. We hold regular after-school activities every Tuesday, providing systematic first aid knowledge learning, forming first aid teams for school sports events, carrying out volunteer services at Min'ai Welfare Institute, organizing AHA first aid certificate training, Mental Health Week and first aid lectures. We also launch new projects including first aid handbook compilation and off-campus community first aid teaching, committed to serving the campus and the public with practical humanitarian actions.
