## 社团介绍

HELPHANT is a student-led organization dedicated to raising awareness of elephant conservation, and broader environmental issues and animal welfare through education, art, and public engagement. Through social media campaigns, educational activities, and charity sales, we aim to help more people understand the challenges facing elephants and encourage meaningful action to support international conservation efforts. Beyond animal protection, HELPHANT also seeks to make community service more inclusive by creating opportunities for people from different backgrounds to participate, express themselves, and contribute to society. We believe that small actions can connect people and create lasting impact.
