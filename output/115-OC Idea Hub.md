## 社团介绍

This ECA is a creative community for students passionate about original characters (OC) design, visual world-building and imaginative expression. Our members will be able to explore how to visualise fictional personas, design their original characters(such as costume designing and story plotting), and present them by hand-drawing. Apart from personal creative development, our group wants to create a society which encourages the members to visualize, improve and share their idea. This community offers some guidelines for building up original worlds and characters, and provides a platform to make us brainstorm together and share our thoughts.
