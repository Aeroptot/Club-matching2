## 社团介绍

Bacamera Filmmaker Club focuses on nourishing SCIEr who desire to make their own films and SCIEr who would like to systematically learn about film theories and the filmmaking process. We are the first filmmaking club that covers everything from theoretical courses and film analysis to practical experience on set in SCIE.
