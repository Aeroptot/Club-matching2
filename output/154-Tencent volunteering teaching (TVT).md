## 社团介绍

TVT (Tencent Volunteer Teaching) is a platform provided by Tencent Volunteer Organization that connects top-performing students with learners in remote areas through online tutoring. Since launching in 2022, TVT has deeply connected outstanding students from top schools in Beijing, Shanghai, Shenzhen, and even from the UK's G5 universities with students in remote areas who are eager to broaden their horizons and improve their academic performance.
