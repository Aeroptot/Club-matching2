## 社团介绍

The Electromagnetics & Optics Society is a theory‑oriented club focused on the fundamental principles of electricity, magnetism, and light. Our sessions cover topics such as Ohm’s law, electric fields, potential energy, Lorentz force, magnetic flux, electromagnetic induction, Lenz’s law, motional and induced EMF, photoelectric effect, double‑slit interference, grating diffraction, and half‑wave loss. We meet weekly for guided discussions and problem‑solving sessions. The emphasis is on building a clear conceptual understanding and working through challenging questions together. Simple demonstrations may be included occasionally to help visualize abstract ideas, but the main focus remains on theoretical reasoning and group discussion. Members are encouraged to ask questions and share their thinking. All students with an interest in physics are welcome, regardless of prior background.
