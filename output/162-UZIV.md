## 社团介绍

UZIV has been part of SCIE for 19 years, dedicated to creating positive change through meaningful community service. Our club believes that small acts of kindness can bring lasting impact, and we design activities that connect students with both local needs and wider social concerns. Over the years, we have organised beach cleanups to protect the environment, created art portrait projects to celebrate and encourage children living with challenges, and visited children’s welfare institutions to bring care and companionship. The Charity Bazaar allows us to build a deeper connection with the charity house, as all of our revenue is donated in support of their work. It reflects our belief in collective effort: when many people come together with care and energy, they can create a kinder and more supportive community for everyone. Beyond these regular initiatives, UZIV also serves as a space for creativity and inspiration. We encourage members to design new events and contribute their own ideas, making the club a dynamic platform for student-led action. Every project we take on reflects the diversity of our members’ passions while staying true to our mission of fostering compassion and responsibility.
