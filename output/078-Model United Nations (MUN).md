## 社团介绍

Model United Nations (MUN) at SCIE offers students an engaging platform to explore global affairs, diplomacy, and international relations. Designed as an academic and lecture-based ECA, weekly sessions provide structured guidance on geopolitics, parliamentary procedure inspired by the United Nations, resolution drafting, and effective public speaking. Members gain a comprehensive understanding of how international bodies operate while analyzing real-world global challenges. In addition to weekly instructional sessions, members have the opportunity to represent our school at external MUN conferences, applying their skills in competitive, multi-school debates. Furthermore, club members actively participate in organizing, planning, and hosting our school's annual MUN conference—a major event that fosters leadership, logistics management, and teamwork. Whether you are a beginner looking to build confidence or an experienced delegate aiming to refine your diplomatic skills, this ECA equips you with critical thinking and leadership abilities for the future.
