## 社团介绍

The SCIE Tennis Club is a student-led sports ECA dedicated to promoting tennis and an active lifestyle within our school community. We welcome players of all skill levels—from beginners learning the basics to advanced players refining their techniques. Weekly training sessions focus on skill development, match practice, and fitness, while friendly intra-club and inter school tournaments provide competitive opportunities throughout the year. Beyond improving tennis abilities, our club cultivates teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship. We also organize social events and exhibitions to grow the tennis culture on campus. Through regular practice and competition, members build lasting friendships, develop leadership skills, and maintain a healthy balance between academics and physical wellbeing.
