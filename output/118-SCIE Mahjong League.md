## 社团介绍

We consider Mahjong as an excellent mind sport. As so, we provide opportunity for student to attend in our monthly competitions, where students can attend freely everyday and cumulate scores. For beginners, we provide lessons of Mahjong of basic rules to techniques both in regular games and specific strategy in tournament, where students will come up to teach others based on professional books. This is also a place for students to meet other people who have similar interests, where we can have fun and reduce stress from studying. In addition, this can also express and intrigue people's interests of traditional cultures.
