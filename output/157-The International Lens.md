## 社团介绍

The International Lens is for students who see a major news story and think, “I know this matters—but what is actually going on?” Our aim is to make politics, economics, and global affairs easier to understand and much more enjoyable to explore. Each week, we will choose a real event, explain the background in beginner-friendly language, and then do something interactive with it. Members might run a mock election, represent different countries in a crisis, investigate a suspicious headline, compete in a geography challenge, or debate what a government should do next. No previous knowledge is required, and nobody should feel embarrassed about asking a “basic” question. Members will also take turns sharing topics they find interesting and helping one another understand difficult ideas. Last year, we built The International Lens website, which includes beginner lessons, country profiles, event explanations, and a forum. The website will remain a useful resource, but the heart of the ECA will be learning, discussing, questioning, and discovering how surprisingly connected the world is.
