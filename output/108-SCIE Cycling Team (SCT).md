## 社团介绍

SCT is a cycling club that aims to help students ride safely, correctly and confidently while enjoying the sport of cycling. We welcome riders of different experience levels and provide guidance on cycling skills, road safety, group riding and basic bike maintenance. We also introduce sports science knowledge, including endurance training, pacing, recovery and fitness management, so members can improve their performance in a healthy and sustainable way. Through regular training and group rides, SCT hopes to promote fitness, teamwork and a long-term interest in cycling.
