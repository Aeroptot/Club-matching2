## 社团介绍

The SCIE Champion League (SCL) is an inclusive, intramural 6-a-side football league dedicated to fostering sportsmanship, teamwork, and healthy competition within the school community. Designed to welcome students of all genders and skill levels—from absolute beginners to advanced players—SCL prioritizes passion and creativity over technical perfection, providing a highly supportive environment for athletic expression. A defining feature of the league is its NBA-inspired draft mechanism. At the beginning of the academic year, all participants undergo a skills assessment followed by a structured draft, ensuring balanced team rosters and highly competitive midday matches. Beyond intramural play, SCL also unites its top talents into a representative squad to compete in friendly matches against other international schools. More than just a sports tournament, SCL acts as a dynamic campus platform that bridges diverse student groups, cultivates school spirit, and promotes holistic development through a shared love of the beautiful game.
