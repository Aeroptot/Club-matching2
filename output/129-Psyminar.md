## 社团介绍

Against the widespread student psychological pressure, this ECA aims to improve students’ health and well-being by targeting the gut-mental health link. Scientific research identifies the gut as the human “second brain”, and imbalanced gut microbiota or impaired gut barrier function can significantly raise stress hormone levels and contribute to anxiety and low mood. Regarding the link between intestinal health and mental wellbeing, this ECA translates science into accessible activities. The ECA aims to raise awareness of nutrition mental health connections via practical exercises and self tracking, thus improving mental wellbeing. In summary, this ECA combines biological knowledge, neuroscience, and psychology with lifestyle management to achieve mental wellbeing via dietary approaches.
