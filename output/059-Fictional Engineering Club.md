## 社团介绍

Fictional Engineering offers weekly lectures and hands-on workshops covering Engineering Design, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, 3D Modelling, and 3D Printing & Rapid Prototyping. Our project-based approach takes students from fundamental engineering concepts and tool usage to designing, prototyping, and building complete projects.
