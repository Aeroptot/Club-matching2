## 社团介绍

The French Film ECA is a space for students to watch and discuss a wide range of French-language films together. Through films from different genres, periods, and French-speaking regions, members will explore French culture, society, history, and cinematic aesthetics. After each film, members will be encouraged to share their personal views and interpretations in a friendly and open discussion. We believe that listening to different opinions can help us notice details and perspectives that we may have missed on our own. Through these discussions, we hope to develop a deeper appreciation of cinema and a more thoughtful way of viewing the world. The ECA welcomes everyone, regardless of their level of French or previous knowledge of film. English subtitles will be provided when necessary. Our main aim is to enjoy French-language cinema together while learning from the films and from one another.
