## 社团介绍

The Entrepreneurship Club aims to introduce students to both the theoretical and practical sides of entrepreneurship. Inspired by my experience at LaunchX, where I studied entrepreneurial concepts and developed a startup with my team, I wanted to create a space at school for students who are interested in business but may not yet have opportunities to explore entrepreneurship in practice. Through interactive workshops, case discussions, and group projects, members learn how to identify market opportunities, conduct market research, develop value propositions and MVPs, understand basic business models and finance, and pitch ideas effectively. Rather than focusing only on lectures, the club encourages students to apply these concepts to real-world problems. For example, members have discussed developing a platform that connects food-delivery services while providing calorie information, personalized dietary planning, and restaurant recommendations for users with fitness goals. Ultimately, the club hopes to help students become more observant, creative, and analytical problem-solvers while gaining practical experience in turning an initial idea into a viable business concept.
