## 社团介绍

Pickleball Club is an ECA run by students. The aim of this club is to introduce students to pickleball and create a fun and inclusive atmosphere for students to exercise, learn, and interact with people. With regular training sessions and friendly matches being conducted, members get a chance to practice the basics of the game, improve their techniques, and work together. The club is open to every student irrespective of whether he/she has a background in the game. Apart from practicing sports, the club intends to promote an active and healthy way of life and create a friendly community. We also hope to organize or participate in competitions with other clubs or communities to encourage students to use their skills in real matches.
