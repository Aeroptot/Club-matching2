## 社团介绍

SCIE Scuba Diving Club is a sports-focused ECA dedicated to promoting scuba diving culture and marine environmental protection. We welcome both PADI-certified divers and ocean-loving students without certification as land volunteers. We run regular sessions on PADI OW foundational theory, diving safety and marine ecosystem knowledge. In summer, we organize certified open-water diving trips to Shenzhen Dapeng, with free equipment including BCD and wetsuits provided. Members can take part in public-welfare dives to clear marine litter, earning community service hours while contributing to ocean conservation. Our club aims to build a community for diving enthusiasts, foster marine protection awareness, and provide safe, well-organized opportunities to explore the underwater world.
