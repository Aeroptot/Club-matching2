## 社团介绍

Musicals not Operas is a passionate, student-led musical theatre community at SCIE, dedicated purely to musical performances and original stage creation. We gather students with genuine enthusiasm and artistic talent for musicals, aiming to build the school’s most outstanding and influential musical club.The club holds weekly rehearsals to refine members’ stage literacy and performance coherence. Our key annual project is the 2027 March Musical Gala, which strings diverse classic musical excerpts into one cohesive stage show. Our club has proven strong independent production capability. We have successfully staged a complete 45-minute original musical entirely run by students, including self-composed music, original lyrics, full costume and makeup design, and independent on-stage performance, marking our mature artistic execution ability.
