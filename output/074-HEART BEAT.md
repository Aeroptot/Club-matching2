## 社团介绍

Our club is open to students from all disciplines who have an interest in understanding human personality psychology. We welcome anyone eager to explore the intricacies of psychology and introduce our club members to a wide range of professional knowledge on psychology, including Typology, personality psychology, analytical psychology and psychodynamic. Our club members are able to enjoy activities including lectures and workshops, as ECA leaders will share their research and insights on various topics related to psychology. Besides, they can also join volunteering projects that helps in explore psychology more in actions. Members can discuss theories with practical examples in reality to gain a better understanding. Our original objectives are to build a comprehensive understanding of psychology and to create an interactive environment where members can discuss and explore various aspects of psychology. Moreover, we also aim to help members gain a deeper understanding of their own personalities and foster personal growth. Join our club to delve into the fascinating world of personality psychology, connect with passionate peers, and listen to the heartbeat from every personality type.
