## 社团介绍

Starting and evolving from SCIE RC Racing Club last year, Apex Racing is a brand new and fully developed RC racing team, that not only focus on engineering and racing, but also teamwork, collaboration skills, and abilities to adapt to new tasks. We need more than tech geeks, finance and public media department needs much more members to join us and fight together. In the ECA we are going to form a real racing team, with its typical structure. Members will have hand-on experience of dealing with a racing season, from the start with preparations, to the end winning the grand prize. During practice, everyone will have the opportunity to drive the 1/10 Touring Car to perform tasks, achieve standard lap time, and learn from the ECA leader about all technical knowledge related to RC racing cars. These knowledges can also be used on real cars. Furthermore, all spendings and costs during the season will rely on sponsors, and thus members do not need to pay for any additional material costs. In collaboration with other ECAs, we will be studying for all ranges of information. Feel free to join and enjoy a wonderful journey with professional Remote controlled cars.
