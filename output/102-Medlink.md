## 社团介绍

We are a biomedical extracurricular ECA for exploring the profound intersection of clinical science, ethics, and humanistic care. We delve deeply into common-seen medical cases, from stroke rehabilitation to euthanasia by first grounding each topic in its biological fundamentals. Our top mission is to understand the full spectrum of medicine—from cells to the bedside—while considering its ethical dimensions, and to practice with compassion. Every member collaborates to create in-depth articles and videos that will be posted on our official platforms to raise public awareness and funds for disability charities. Beyond theory, we bridge learning to practice through hospital and hospice shadowing.
