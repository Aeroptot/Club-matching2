## 社团介绍

The Science Newsletter Club (SNC) is an ECA dedicated to promoting scientific knowledge and curiosity within our school community. Through our regular newsletters, we will introduce a wide range of scientific topics, from interesting facts and everyday phenomena to recent discoveries and developments in science. Members will have the opportunity to research scientific topics, develop their scientific communication and writing skills, and collaborate to create informative content for the wider school community. The club aims to encourage students to explore the world of science beyond the classroom and foster curiosity.
