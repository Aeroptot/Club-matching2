## 社团介绍

SCIE Vocaloid Club explores Japanese Vocaloid music and culture through music appreciation, analysis, and discussions on iconic virtual singers, producers, and the evolution of Vocaloid technology. We also explore the development of Vocaloid music culture, providing a welcoming platform for members to share interests, deepen their understanding, and connect with fellow Vocaloid enthusiasts.
