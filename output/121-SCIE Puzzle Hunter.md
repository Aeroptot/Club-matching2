## 社团介绍

Puzzle Hunt is an event where teams compete to solve a series of puzzles. It originated from MIT in 1981 and has now become one of the most popular events in universities. Puzzle Hunt is a community with diversity. Everyone can find their excellent field. In SCIE Puzzle Hunter, students will delve into different types of puzzles: logic puzzles, crosswords, cryptography, steganography, detective fiction, linguistics, etc. Through this process, we will explore useful techniques that can be used in puzzle hunt: searching, frequency analysis, brute force machines, solvers, etc. We will try past puzzle hunts like MIT Mystery Hunt and Microsoft Puzzle Hunt to feel the charm of those exciting puzzle structures and designs. Furthermore, we will form teams to participate in those ongoing hunts and compete with teams from all over the world. Like those hunts held by other universities, our ultimate goal is to write our own SCIE Puzzle Hunt in this year.
