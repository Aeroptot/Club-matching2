## 社团介绍

The Shenzhen International Student Football Fans Club has operated for one academic year and is applying to continue its work. We are a student-led football community that connects SCIE students with local football culture through media, discussion, and supporter activities. During our first year, we produced and published original football content on our WeChat account, including filmed interviews with Shenzhen Juniors Football Club striker Arnold Garita and coach Ramiro Amarelle. These interviews explored players’ professional journeys, training, adapting to football in Shenzhen, and the work behind a local club. We planned questions, conducted interviews, filmed and edited videos, created bilingual subtitles, and shared the final content with students online. This year, we hope to continue documenting Shenzhen football while building a welcoming community for students who enjoy the sport.
