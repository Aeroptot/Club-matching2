## 社团介绍

SCIE Philosophy Club is a discussion-based ECA for students interested in examining everyday questions more carefully. Each session begins with a question, thought experiment, short reading, film clip, or real-world case, followed by student-led discussion and argument. Topics may include free will, personal identity, morality, knowledge, language, aesthetics, political philosophy, and questions raised by new technologies such as artificial intelligence. Rather than requiring previous knowledge of philosophy, the club will introduce philosophical ideas when they become useful for understanding the question at hand. Members will learn to identify assumptions, construct arguments, respond to objections, and revise their views through discussion. We hope to create an open environment in which disagreement is treated as something to investigate rather than something that has to be resolved immediately.
