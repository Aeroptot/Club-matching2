## 社团介绍

This year, "University Physics" isn't just about memorizing equations—it's about fundamentally changing how we see the universe. We’ll step beyond the standard high school curriculum, moving from the familiar Newtonian world of pushing and pulling forces into the beautifully elegant realm of analytical mechanics. By adopting the Lagrangian approach, we’ll explore the principle of least action, discovering how complex systems can be understood entirely through energy rather than messy vectors. But we won't stop at classical mechanics. We’re also diving into the weird, wonderful frontiers of modern physics. Through concepts like quantum mechanics and special relativity, we’ll unpack mind-bending, counterintuitive ideas that perfectly explain the bizarre realities of our world. At its core, our club is about building genuine physical and mathematical intuition. We want to explore advanced problem-solving methods and theorems you won't find in standard textbooks. By tackling these challenges together, we'll equip you with the strong intuition and deep understanding needed to confidently pursue higher-level physics in the future.
