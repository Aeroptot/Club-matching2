## 社团介绍

As one of SCIE's largest and oldest finance clubs (over 10 years of history), SCIE Finance Club (SFC) offers something for everyone—it is built for the ones who are passionate about finance and investing, or simply aiming for a prosperous future. The finance lectures we offered are guaranteed to broaden students’ horizons and give them valuable insights students won't find elsewhere. Our lecture content includes but not limited to compound interest, risk-return tradeoff, candlestick and even behavioral finance. More importantly, the core numbers will lead group discussions about the breaking financial news updated frequently, to help students understand how the financial markets work.
