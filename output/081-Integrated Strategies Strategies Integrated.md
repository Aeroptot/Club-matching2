## 社团介绍

Integrated Strategies Strategies Integrated is an ECA that explores how history can be learned, represented and debated through video games, board games and strategy games. Members will look at how games set in different periods, such as Ancient Rome, medieval kingdoms, the World Wars or the Cold War, present historical events, leaders, societies and conflicts. We will discuss what games get right, what they simplify, and how game design influences our understanding of the past. Students will also try short challenges such as analysing historical accuracy, comparing games with real sources, and designing their own mini history game concepts. This ECA combines academic discussion with interactive activities, making history more engaging and memorable. It is suitable for students who enjoy history, gaming, storytelling, strategy and critical thinking.
