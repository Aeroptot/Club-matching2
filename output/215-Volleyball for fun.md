## 社团介绍

The ECA is called haikyu volleyball for fun eca. Its purpose is to help students to get in-touched to volleyball or let students practice their volleyball skills. Regular tutorials will be given through the sessions. The aim of this eca is for new students that’s interested in volleyball to actually try and learn new skills and also provide a place for students who already know several skills in volleyball, to practice.
