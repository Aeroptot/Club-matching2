## 社团介绍

Skiing Society is a student-run community for skiers and snowboarders of all levels. We are dedicated to creating a supportive, peer-driven environment where members help each other improve, regardless of experience. Every weekend (Saturday or Sunday), the club organises trips to Huafa Indoor Ski Resort in Shenzhen for regular training sessions. Beginners are warmly welcomed—experienced members provide guidance on fundamentals, while more advanced riders share tips on technique and control. Through mutual support and consistent practice, every member can progress safely and confidently. During school holidays, the club also arranges group ski trips to outdoor resorts, offering opportunities to explore real mountains and strengthen friendships beyond campus. Whether you are a complete beginner or an experienced skier, SCIE Ski Club is a place to learn, grow, and share your passion for snow sports. Join us and take your first run with a community that has your back.
