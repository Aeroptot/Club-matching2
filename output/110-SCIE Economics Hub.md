## 社团介绍

SCIE Economics Hub is an economics society that introduces students to the subject and guides those preparing to study economics at the university level. We focus on developing an intuitive understanding of the conceptual frameworks central to modern economic theory. We use mathematical modeling where helpful to illustrate key economic methods and formal definitions. We welcome all students interested in economics to join us, explore the subject, and share ideas with a cohort of passionate and intellectually curious peers.
