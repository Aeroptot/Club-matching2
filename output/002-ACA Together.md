## 社团介绍

A Cappella Club is a student-led vocal ensemble where members create music entirely through the human voice, without instrumental accompaniment. The club welcomes students with different levels of singing experience and provides a supportive environment for members to develop their vocal and performance skills. During weekly sessions, members will learn fundamental a cappella techniques, including vocal warm-ups, breathing, resonance, harmony, blending, rhythm, and ensemble listening. Members will be assigned suitable voice parts based on their vocal ranges and will work together to rehearse arrangements for school performances such as SGT and other events throughout the year. Beyond preparing for performances, the club aims to build a close and collaborative musical community. Through rehearsals, section practices, and performances, members will learn not only how to become more confident singers, but also how to listen, communicate, and contribute effectively as part of an ensemble.
