## 社团介绍

This ECA is established for students who are interested in different culture. The sessions that this ECA will have will related to Anthropology, Sociology, regional study, cultural study and Philosophy. The topic of this semester will be about eastern Asia’s culture . All the knowledge of the culture is according to books like ‘The Chrysanthemum and Sword’ ‘OTAKU: Japan’s database animal’, ‘From the soil’ and ‘the Individualization of Chinese Society’. We are going to include the transformation of society, the reason why cultures are different, and most importantly, we will have a more objective and more anthropological insight when we are facing cultural differences. The form of the sessions will mainly be teaching with PPT. To make sure the concepts are understandable, there will be many real life examples which are close to daily life. Also, there will be many interactions during the sessions, so that our members can discuss our different ideas.
