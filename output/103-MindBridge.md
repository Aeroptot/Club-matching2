## 社团介绍

MindBridge is a peer-driven forum for students passionate about mental health and clinical science. We explore core topics including psychopathology, evidence-based interventions, diagnostic systems, ethics, and the scientist‑practitioner model. Through interactive case discussions, journal article reviews, role‑play demonstrations, and occasional guest speakers, members gain a realistic preview of clinical psychology training and practice. Our sessions foster critical thinking, cultural humility, and the integration of research with real‑world clinical work. We aim to build a supportive community where curiosity about human behaviour meets rigorous scientific inquiry, helping members prepare for further study or careers in mental health. The ECA values diversity, empathy, and evidence‑based approaches, while also encouraging self‑reflection and personal growth. We welcome anyone interested in understanding psychological distress and healing, regardless of prior background, and we strive to reduce stigma around mental health through open, respectful dialogue.
