## 社团介绍

Girl Up is a student-led club dedicated to promoting gender equality and creating a space for students to learn, discuss, and take action on issues affecting girls and women. Through discussions during club session, volunteering work with non-profit communities and yearly summits, we hope to turn awareness into meaningful action. Whether you are passionate about gender equality or simply curious to learn more, Girl Up is a place to share your voice, hear different perspectives, and make a difference within and beyond our school community.
