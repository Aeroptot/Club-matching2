## 社团介绍

Our club focus on the contemporary and traditional cultural expressions across East Asia, especially on literature, comics, visual storytelling, fan culture, and other forms of popular media. Through close reading and cross-cultural discussion, members will examine how cultural works reflect the cultural environments in which they are created. The club aims to explore the broader ideas and social changes embedded within them, while also considering similarities and differences across different regions and cultural contexts.
