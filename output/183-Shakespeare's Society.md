## 社团介绍

At Shakespeare’s Society we aim to help IGCSE and A-level students attain skills to further their creative writing. Creative writing is a crucial part of English language and is most times an overlooked and underperforming part of exams for students. From the structure to the use of imaginative vocabulary, we hope to give a better and more in-depth outline of creative writing, as well as plenty of practice and examples, which will help students reach their full potential of storytelling.
