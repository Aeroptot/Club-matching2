## 社团介绍

SCIE ML×AI is a hands-on AI and data science ECA open to students with or without prior machine-learning experience. It develops members through three connected areas: Data Science, using Kaggle competitions to learn data analysis, validation, modelling and submission; AI Olympiads, using NOAI/IOAI-style problems to build deep-learning intuition and problem-solving insight; and Agentic Engineering, using prompts(a.k.a Agentic Skills), coding agents and multi-agent systems to implement AI models efficiently. No prior experience with coding is required as members will not copy code line by line. AI tools handle much of the implementation, while students define tasks, run experiments, evaluate results and decide how to improve their models. Seven guided sessions establish shared foundations in agent setup, tabular modelling, NLP, computer vision, Transformers and multimodal learning. The ECA then continues as a collaborative problem-solving lab using real datasets and competition tasks. Members may work independently or in teams, prepare competition submissions and present their methods, results and lessons to the school community.
