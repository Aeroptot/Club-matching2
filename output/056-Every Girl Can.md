## 社团介绍

Every Girl Can (EGC) is the largest girls’ football club at Shenzhen College of International Education (SCIE), providing a welcoming and supportive space for girls to develop their football skills and passion for the game. Whether you are an experienced player or have never played football before, EGC offers professional training designed to help every member fully develop her skills, build confidence, and enjoy the excitement of playing and competing. Beyond football, it is also a place to meet like-minded young female footballers, make new friends, and become part of a supportive community.
