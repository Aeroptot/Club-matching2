## 社团介绍

This is a club that aims to provide introductory undergraduate level physics course for students interested in physics, especially electricity and magnetism. This course is specially designed with a blend of in depth physics with real life interactive demonstrations that allows students to observe learn physics in reality intuitively. We will be following the greats of the physcis history, building equipments from scratch: E.g battery out of copper coins and vinega, derive Gauss's law and use it to prove where charge sits on a conductor, and by the end of the course studnets will hopefully master three out of the four Maxwell Equations .No prior knowledge is assumed in this course(basic algebra understanding in needed, preknowledge to calculas may be helpful, but will be covered).
