## 社团介绍

This is a geology club where students can explore the forces and processes that shape the Earth. Each secession begins with an intriguing earth mystery. Why do earthquakes and volcanoes occur in plate boundaries? How can seismic waves reveal Earth’s interior? Why are some mountains red? How do glaciers create fjords, and how can ocean temperatures affect weather around the world? We will first go through the basic knowledge of geology such as rock cycle and sedimentary processes. Then we will talk about interesting geographical phenomena and landscapes. Some topics and activities will be selected through member voting, allowing the club to reflect students’ interests. We will use maps, photographs, rock samples, models, real data and group challenges to examine evidence and develop explanations.
