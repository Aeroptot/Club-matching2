## 社团介绍

As one of the biggest ECA on campus, this club is dedicated to preparing students for the High School Mathematical Contest in Modeling (HiMCM) by COMAP, a globally recognized academic competition. Our mission is to equip members with advanced mathematical knowledge and skills, extending beyond the classroom, to solve complex real-world problems creatively. The club offers training in mathematical modeling, data analysis, and report writing, enhancing reasoning, teamwork, and innovation. Members will learn from past winners, work with experienced mentors( including alumni), and participate in simulated competitions, all aimed at excelling on an international stage. We actively participate in schools themed week and activities, and have won the "Most Creative ECA"(2/380+) in 24, and the “Most Popular ECA” in 25, with 2 teams of members won a M prize and 1 team won the F prize in HiMCM, one team won F prize in IMMC.
