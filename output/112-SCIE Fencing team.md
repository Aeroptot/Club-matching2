## 社团介绍

Our club brings fencing to campus through engaging activities with a strong emphasis on actual bouting. We organize practice matches and friendly competitions, helping members learn by doing. Everyone can find a suitable sparring partner and gain valuable experience through live fencing. Our goal is to create a vibrant community where students enjoy the thrill of real combat while developing their skills in a safe and encouraging atmosphere.
