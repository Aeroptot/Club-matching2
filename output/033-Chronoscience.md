## 社团介绍

Chronoscience is a history club which aims explore the intersection between history and technology. Rather than treating history simply as a collection of past events, the club examines how technologies have shaped human connection, migration, communication, conflict, and collective memory, as well as how digital tools can change the ways we research and present history.
