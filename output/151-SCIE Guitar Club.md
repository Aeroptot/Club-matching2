## 社团介绍

SCIE Guitar Club is a student‑led hobby‑oriented community open to all students, regardless of prior guitar experience. We welcome absolute beginners who have never touched an instrument, as well as intermediate and advanced guitar players. Our club creates a relaxed, supportive space for students to share passion for string‑instrument music. Members can practice acoustic or electric guitar, exchange playing tips, learn popular songs, and jam with peers. We encourage mutual help: experienced players will share basic techniques for new members. We aim to build a friendly musical community where students can unwind from academic pressure and connect with others who love guitar music.
