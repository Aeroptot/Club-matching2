## 社团介绍

Formerly Introduction to Architecture, we broaden the content and aim to dismantle the traditional barriers between STEM and the Arts. Inspired by the interdisciplinary mindset championed by top universities like Harvard and Cambridge, we create a platform where members explore the connection between diverse fields like art, science, history, and technology. We hold lectures, discussions and hands-on activities, displaying our members’ work through an exhibition. Our curriculum includes learning foundational modelling skills (Blender, Rhino) and engaging in themed interdisciplinary lectures and experiments. We provide a collaborative and 100% English environment where beginners can master modelling tools, exchange ideas, and broaden their horizons.
