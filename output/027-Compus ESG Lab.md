## 社团介绍

Our club is dedicated to imparting to students some basic concepts about ESG. Meanwhile, we will use specific company cases to help students understand and train them to apply the abstract notions in practice. Furthermore, each semester, our club will select one campus or community issue, such as an investigation into food waste in the school canteen, to publish a report. Additionally, it is essential to equip members with literacy in dissecting ESG reports and investigative skills, such as visualisation of data and scoring & rubric design. Overall, our club is created to educate students to have a mindset that includes environmental concerns, social welfare and justice in administration when they are making financial and economic decisions for sustainability.
