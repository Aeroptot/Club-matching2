## 社团介绍

Math and Engineering Teaching Club intend to deliver high-quality lessons in various subjects such as math, chemistry, physics, computer science and economics to primary school students from school such as Green Oasis School. We will pre-prepare concise teaching syllabus and power point according to the interest of our members and the intention of students. Each week, we will go teaching offline, conduct creative acticities and record our teaching/learning outcome through various ways such as poster, student presentation, debate and etc.
