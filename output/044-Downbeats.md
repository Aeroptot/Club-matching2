We aim to develop our members' instrumental proficiency, stage presence, and ability to perform as a cohesive unit. We prepare for school events, concerts, and competitions, fostering a sense of community and shared passion for music. The band is a platform for members to express themselves musically, build confidence, and contribute to the school's vibrant cultural life.
