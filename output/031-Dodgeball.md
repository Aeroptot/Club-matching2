## 社团介绍

The dodgeball club is an organization dedicated to promoting the sport of dodgeball among students at our school. Our club's activities include weekly practices where members can work on their throwing and catching skills, as well as strategy and teamwork. We organize intramural games and pick-up games, which allow members to play in a more casual setting. Additionally, the members of the dodgeball club will be able to participate in the once-in-a-year dodgeball house event, providing our members with the opportunity to showcase their abilities and test their mettle against other players. Overall, our goal is to provide a fun and inclusive environment for students to enjoy the game of dodgeball, while also helping them to improve their physical fitness and teamwork abilities.
