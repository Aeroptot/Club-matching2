## 社团介绍

The English Teaching Club is a student-led service club dedicated to making English learning more accessible, engaging, and enjoyable for younger students. Through volunteer teaching, interactive activities, and educational outreach, club members create lessons tailored to students’ different learning needs and interests. Beyond teaching English, ETC aims to build meaningful connections between students and the wider community. Members develop lesson-planning, communication, leadership, and teamwork skills while encouraging younger learners to gain confidence in using English. Through regular classes, community outreach, and creative learning activities, the club strives to make language education more inclusive and empowering.
