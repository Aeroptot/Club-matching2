## 社团介绍

We aim to build a highly skilled Chinese debate team that proudly represents SCIE. We welcome all students who are interested in or already skilled in Chinese debating, and will select a core team through tryouts. Our goal is to carve out a space for SCIE on the Chinese debating stage, and we firmly believe that in the future, Chinese debate will become one of SCIE’s shining hallmarks.
