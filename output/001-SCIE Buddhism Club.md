## 社团介绍

SCIE Buddhist Wisdom Club introduces classical Buddhist ideas in a simple and open way. Members study compassion, karma, anger, meditation, and emptiness through selected texts and school-life examples. Teachers and students can ask questions and join respectful discussions. People of all beliefs are welcome; no religious commitment is required.
