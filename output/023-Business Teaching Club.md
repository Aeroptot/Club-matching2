## 社团介绍

The Business Teaching Club is the club that can offer members the opportunity to gain practical insights into theoretical business knowledge. Furthermore, they have the chance to express this valuable information by voluntarily teaching primary school students. Whether one is an experienced entrepreneur, an aspiring business leader, or a curious student, our club serves as a dynamic platform for expanding both their business acumen and their contributions to others' knowledge.
