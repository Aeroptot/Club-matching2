## 社团介绍

The Policy Insight ECA will focus on the subject of public policy. We invite students who are interested in policy, sociology, economics, or just want to dive into the policy around us. Each week, we will select one concrete, relevant case study close to our daily lives, ranging from our school to our community, and even to the whole society, focusing on policy or creating potential policy that might be helpful. We may have opportunities to implement policies. Students will not only learn the theory but also analyze or debate the policy. In this club, anyone who is interested in policy can be the lecturer, raise their doubts, and invite peers to discuss. Our final goal is to turn our daily observations and knowledge into practical outcomes, giving members a platform to contribute to their surroundings.
