## 社团介绍

Our aim is to provide information about acupuncture and traditional Chinese medicines to students who are interested. We also teach the students how to diagnose and how to treat every-day injuries using a unique traditional Chinese method. Further more, we also do service learning by using our knowledge to benefit our community family, friends and social groups.
