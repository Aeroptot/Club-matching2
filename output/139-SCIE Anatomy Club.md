## 社团介绍

The Anatomy Club aims to provide students with a deeper understanding of human anatomy and biological structures through both theoretical learning and hands-on practical activities. Members will explore topics such as the nervous system, respiratory system, circulatory system and other human organs/ systems. The club also conducts practical sessions using animal specimens, including frogs and pig organs such as the kidney, lung, and brain, to help members connect anatomical knowledge with real biological structures. This year, we also hope to introduce basic suturing practice to expand members’ understanding of practical medical techniques.
