## 社团介绍

Students will create handmade accessories and use the mandala as a base to create ornaments. We will explore creativity, focus, and self-expression through the art of mandala design. Using drawing, colouring, painting and recycled materials, we will create unique mandala artworks while developing collaborative skills and artistic confidence.
