## 社团介绍

The Animal Science and Welfare Club is a student-led club exploring animal biology, behaviour, health, and welfare. Through discussions, presentations, case studies, interactive activities, and research, members will learn about how animals function, behave, and interact with humans. The club also examines real-world issues such as responsible animal care, conservation, animal ethics, and the challenges faced by animals in different environments. Our aim is to combine scientific knowledge with empathy and encourage students to develop a deeper understanding of animals and our responsibilities towards them.
