## 社团介绍

Our ECA is an astronomy outreach club that aims to make astronomy accessible, engaging, and relevant to students with different levels of scientific background. Through regular student-led sessions, we introduce topics ranging from neutron stars, stellar evolution, and auroras to asteroid defense, Mars exploration, and the future of human spaceflight. Rather than focusing on highly technical theory, our sessions emphasize clear explanations, visual materials, discussion, and curiosity-driven exploration. A distinctive part of our ECA is our astronomy photography program. We have access to remotely operated telescopes located at astronomical observatories in Ningxia and other regions of China. Members can apply for observing time and use these telescopes to capture astronomical objects under the guidance of the current and former club presidents. This gives students an opportunity to move beyond learning about astronomy and actually observe and photograph the universe themselves. We also organize an annual visit to the Shenzhen Astronomical Observatory, where members can experience professional astronomical facilities and learn about the daily work of astronomers.
