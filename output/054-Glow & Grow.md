## 社团介绍

Glow & Grow is a student-led club that fosters confidence, wellness, and creativity within the school community. The teenage years are often marked by self-doubt, insecurities, and pressure from societal standards. Our club provides a safe and uplifting environment where students can strengthen their psychological resilience, developing a healthier relationship with themselves and others, and also providing professional guidance to enhance self-care skills, therefore enhancing confidence. Through activities in confidence-building, journaling, self-expression and stress management, our eca “Glow & Grow” aims to spread positivity throughout the school’s atmosphere. By helping members feel comfortable in their own appearance and encouraging self-acceptance, the club contributes to a more supportive, empathetic, and confident student body. The ultimate goal is not only personal growth for members, but also the creation of a school-wide culture of encouragement, self-belief, and community care.
