## 社团介绍

"To-Listen" is an Extra-Curricular Activity (ECA)/Project Group dedicated to providing online peer counseling and support for teenagers. Our group offers a safe and confidential space where members can engage in meaningful conversations, seek guidance, and receive support from their peers. The core philosophy of "To-Listen" revolves around active listening, empathy, and fostering a supportive community environment.
