## 社团介绍

Our ECA focuses on neurodiversity and cognitive accessibility in school environments. We will introduce the concept of "cognitive variations" that result from genetic differences, the environments in which people grow up, and differences in how people think, and then explore how these variations may influence students’ experiences in school. Then, we will explain and examine cognitive barriers, such as ADHD, dyslexia, and autism, that can arise from everyday learning materials, classroom environments, and communication methods. Each session will focus on a different aspect of neurodiversity or cognitive accessibility, such as attention, memory, reading, sensory processing, or executive function. The most important component of CogniAble ECA is to investigate practical ways to reduce them. The ECA aims to connect concepts from cognitive science with practical changes in the school environment, with a focus on making information and everyday school experiences more cognitively accessible.
