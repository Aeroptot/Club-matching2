## 社团介绍

Quicksilver studio has not only been a music club, but the heart of SCIE’s musical expression. As one of the most representative music clubs, we aim for every music lover to find a place to express and showcase, to meet someone with the same hobby, to encounter new and fun people from different ages and backgrounds, or to simply find a place to enjoy music and harmony. Quicksilver is a community built on mutual respect, friendship, and a shared, burning passion for music. We are "equal friends" where every voice is heard, and every unique style is encouraged. Our goal is to create a safe, supportive, and inspiring environment where members can be their authentic selves, shine in their own way, and, most importantly, play the “coolest music” with reliable people. We meet to share music, sing songs, and perform our souls out.
