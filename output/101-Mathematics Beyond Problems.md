## 社团介绍

This is a student-led mathematics ECA focused on understanding mathematics beyond simply applying formulas to solve problems. The main focus will be on Further Mathematics and AS/AL Mathematics, with sessions exploring where mathematical concepts and formulas come from, why they work, and how different ideas are connected. Topics may also be selected according to members’ interests. Sessions will generally include teaching, discussion, problem solving, and mathematical exploration. Active members will also be encouraged to prepare and lead sessions on topics they are interested in.
