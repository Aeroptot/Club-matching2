## 社团介绍

The Biobío club is a student-led extracurricular activity dedicated to exploring biology through hands-on activities, field observation, and interdisciplinary projects. Rather than focusing on lectures or examination preparation, the club aims to make biology engaging and relevant to everyday life. Activities will include campus biodiversity surveys, species identification, and investigations into the biology behind familiar experiences such as plant pigments, fragrances, food, and nutrition. By connecting ecology, environmental science, molecular biology, and nutrition, members will explore the diversity of biological sciences while developing their own interests. The club welcomes students with different levels of biological knowledge and encourages curiosity, collaboration, and independent exploration.
