## 社团介绍

The National History ECA will focus on preparing the students for the National History Day competition which is usually held in March at Concordia International School Shanghai. In preparation, students will conduct their own research and create a website on the Historical theme of 'Innovation in History: Impact, Influence, Change.' Students will then present their research to a panel of judges on the day of the competition. Winners of the initial competition will have the opportunity to display their projects in the finals in the United States! The ECA will provide an introduction to the NHD competition, and ongoing support for your project
