## 社团介绍

Earth Three-body Organization was founded in 2023. Inspired by the sci-fi "The Three-body Problem," this community was founded to gather enthusiasts of the book, given its widespread influence around the globe. After reforms, we held weekly seminars discussing the literary, scientific and humanitarian aspects of the novel. Everyone will have a nametag, and will be invited by the host to share their thoughts in the seminars. Such activity format was inspired by US liberal arts based education, valuing interdisciplinary, freedom of expression and belonging. Apart from weekly activities, ETO also held projects. In 2024, we attempted to invite the book's author, Mr. Cixin Liu, to give speech at SCIE, but received a kind rejection. Last year, we successfully invited the department head of Astronomy of Sun Yat-sen Univeristy, Prof. Jun Cui, to speak at SCIE, making tangible community impact. Next year, we will be focusing on publishing and creating an archive to the content of our discussion
