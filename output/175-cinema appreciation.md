## 社团介绍

This ECA aims to create a space where people in the school feel free to discuss about films with potential strangers in the school face-to-face. The discussions include the individuals' opinions of the themes they recognize, the director intention and how a certain frame gives the audience a certain feeling etc. The basic procedure is the context of the movie then clips of it then discussion.
