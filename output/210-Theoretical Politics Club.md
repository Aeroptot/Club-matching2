## 社团介绍

Theoretical Politics Club is a discussion-based ECA for students interested in political theory, comparative politics, and the functioning of different political systems. In the first several sessions, members will be introduced to major political theories and concepts, such as liberalism, conservatism, socialism, realism, nationalism, democracy, authoritarianism, legitimacy, state power, and political institutions. After building this theoretical foundation, the club will move toward case-based analysis. Members will examine selected historical and contemporary political regimes, comparing how institutions, ideology, leadership, economic conditions, and social forces shape political outcomes. Sessions will mainly consist of short presentations, guided discussion, debates, and group analysis. The purpose is not to promote any particular political position, but to encourage members to understand political systems from multiple perspectives and develop evidence-based arguments.
