## 社团介绍

The Figure Skating Club is a student-led ECA for students interested in figure skating and sports health. During weekly sessions, members will learn about skating techniques, sports rehabilitation, injury prevention, recovery, and mobility. Once or twice each semester, members who are available will have the opportunity to go ice skating together. The club aims to combine the enjoyment of skating with practical knowledge about staying healthy and active in sports.
