## 社团介绍

The Aerodynamics Club is for students interested in understanding how air flows around objects and how these principles are applied in engineering. This semester, the club will introduce key topics such as fluid properties, pressure, viscosity, boundary layers, lift, drag, flow separation, and airfoil design. Sessions will combine short explanations with demonstrations, discussions, and practical activities so that members can connect physical theory with real aerodynamic applications. As the semester progresses, students will also be introduced to computational fluid dynamics (CFD), learning how computer simulations can be used to visualise and analyse airflow. Depending on members’ experience and interest, we will explore software such as OpenFOAM and PyFR and conduct simple aerodynamic modelling projects. No previous knowledge of aerodynamics or coding is required. The club is designed to be accessible to beginners while also offering opportunities for more experienced students to explore advanced topics. Our goal is to develop members’ understanding of physics, engineering, numerical modelling, and real-world aerodynamic design.
