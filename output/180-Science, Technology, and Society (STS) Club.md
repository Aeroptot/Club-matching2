## 社团介绍

The Science, Technology, and Society (STS) Club - "Innovating Today, Transforming Tomorrow" - serves as a dynamic platform for students to explore the intricate relationships between scientific advancements, technological innovations, and their societal impacts. Our mission is to foster critical thinking and interdisciplinary collaboration, empowering students to understand and address contemporary global challenges through the lens of STS. In addition to engaging discussions and research, the club encourages students to form teams and incubate STS-related projects that leverage technology to create positive societal change. These projects may focus on areas such as sustainable development, social equity, ethical technology, and public health. The STS Club aims to cultivate a generation of socially responsible innovators equipped to navigate and shape the future of our interconnected world. Furthermore, the club aspires to publish two issues of an STS journal annually. This initiative will provide students with the opportunity to engage in academic writing, peer review processes, and the publication of original research and essays. By contributing to this journal, members will gain practical experience in scholarly communication and deepen their understanding of the interdisciplinary nature of STS.
