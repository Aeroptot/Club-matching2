## 社团介绍

The Grove is a student-led creative community that aims to create a welcoming space where students can connect, explore new experiences and feel free to be themselves. In a busy school environment, students may sometimes feel stressed, disconnected or unsure where they belong. The Grove provides a supportive environment where students can build meaningful connections through shared experiences and collaborative activities. Rather than focusing on one fixed hobby, The Grove encourages members to explore different interests and create experiences together. Activities may include creative workshops, discussions, film sessions, hands-on projects and other ideas proposed by members. While activities can change, the experience remains the same: connecting with others, creating something together and growing as a community. Most importantly, members are not simply participants. They are encouraged to contribute ideas, take responsibility and gradually lead projects. Through this process, students develop communication, collaboration, organization and leadership skills in an authentic environment. Like a grove formed by many individual trees, The Grove grows through the unique identities and contributions of every member.
