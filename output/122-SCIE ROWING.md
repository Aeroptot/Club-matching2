## 社团介绍

SCIE Rowing is a club founded in 2021, we believe that Rowing is not only a sport that improves personal performance, but also a test of perseverance and teamwork ability. Rowing is also highly respected in prestigious schools in Britain and the United States, and most schools have their ownrowing teams. For students who yearn for Oxford and Cambridge, rowing is even more an elitesport with a centuries-old cultural heritage, because the most famous sports event between the two universities is the Oxford-Cambridge Boat Race.
