## 社团介绍

Our ECA aims to stimulate students' interest in Chemistry. We will introduce a wide range of topics, including inorganic, organic and physical Chemistry. The seniors will be delivering detailed knowledge about each topic every week, thus enriching our members' knowledge of the subject and enabling them a perceptive understanding of the world of Chemistry. Our lab session every semester will enhance members interest through a series of simple but eye-opening experiments, which will be under the guidance of our supervisor, thus also ensuring the safety of the safety of this process. We won ECA award in two consecutive years.
