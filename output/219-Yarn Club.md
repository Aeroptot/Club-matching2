## 社团介绍

In this club, we will focus on yarn crafts, as the name implies. Members will learn how to crochet, starting with basic techniques such as pulling loops through loops using a hook, and gradually progressing to create intricate items like decorations, dolls, and clothing. We are planning to do more creative work, including exhibitions, collaborations on charitable projects, and crochet workshops for children.
