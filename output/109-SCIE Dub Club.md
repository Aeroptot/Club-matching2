## 社团介绍

The SCIE Dub Club (SDC) offers students an engaging platform to explore the art of voice acting and dubbing. Through weekly sessions, members practice dubbing selected scenes from animations, movies, and TV series in a relaxed and supportive environment. The club focuses on developing vocal flexibility, emotional expression, and technical skills—including voice modulation for different character types . Members also learn about script adaptation, audio synchronization, and basic sound editing. SDC encourages collaboration and creativity, allowing students to perform individually or in groups, share feedback, and improve together. Whether for fun, skill-building, or connecting with like-minded peers, the club aims to cultivate confidence, linguistic ability, and an appreciation for performance arts. No prior experience is required—only enthusiasm for storytelling through voice.
