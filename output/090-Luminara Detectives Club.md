## 社团介绍

This is a platform for all detective fans in SCIE.Here you can learn detective knowledge that you might ever know, we provide lectures about detective culture and criminology (such as Criminal Psychology Lesson and Detective culture history).However the club is more about interest rather than theorist things! We play logic-reasoning games and hold Detective novel discussion regularly.Sometimes we watch detective movies together. Moreover we will plan some activity face to whole school like the detective game that we did last year on Halloween Night.
