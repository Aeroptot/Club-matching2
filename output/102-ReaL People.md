## 社团介绍

ReaL People is a student-led game-development community that has been running for two years. During this time, members have worked together to turn ideas into playable experiences through writing, art, design, programming, audio, translation, testing, and production planning. Our major project, Preface, is an original narrative game developed collaboratively through shared work on worldbuilding, branching dialogue, interactive design, character and environment concepts, models, bilingual text, and playtesting. Our club records document this process across pages for story structure, interaction lists, character development, dialogue, translation, and visual assets. This year, we will continue as a practical creative studio where students learn through small team projects, structured feedback, and iterative improvement.
