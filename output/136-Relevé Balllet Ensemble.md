## 社团介绍

Relevé Ballet Ensemble is a student-led dance ECA designed for students who love ballet and seek opportunities to dance on the stage. The ECA provides opportunities for students to develop ballet techniques and artistic expression in a supportive and collaborative environment. Through regular activities, members will be introduced to ballet works from different choreographers, styles, and historical periods, developing a broader understanding of this art form. In addition, we will explore choreography and performance, allowing members to work together to create dance pieces and perform at events like SCIE Got Talent and New Year Concert.
