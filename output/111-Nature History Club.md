## 社团介绍

The Nature History Club is an ECA in the Natural Science category. Students interested in biological science and the history of science meet up once a week to explore topics beyond the textbook’s level in depth. Usually, the ECA leaders give lectures to the students around a certain biology-related area (e.g., Applied Evolution) and the history of science, while animated videos and fun questions are included to make the lecture more intriguing. There will sometimes be extra activities such as biological experiments taken into practice occasionally during the ECA’s activity time. The Nature History Club is also involved in constructing the SCIE Garden and Greenhouse. We spend a couple of ECA sessions per semester to work on the SCIE Greenhouse, aiming to study plants habitats, investigate plant growing techniques, and plant seeds by ourselves in the SCIE Greenhouse. Our efforts on the greenhouse will be recorded in our greenhouse journals, which might be published in the future. The Nature History Club encourages students to voice out their own thoughts and be the lecturer. We will spare several lessons for these lecturer students.
