## 社团介绍

DSM is a club that intends to focus on the behavioural and developmental aspects of psychology. We aim to expand students' knowledge of various types of psychological experiments by incorporating literature and media into the lessons. The club seeks to explore the psychological theories and disorders reflected in literary and media characters, and explain the experiments done by past psychologists involved with the disorder (e.g Freud with Harry Potter dreams, or tie Pavlov to 1984 by George Orwell). At the end of every session, the club leader and members are encouraged to come up with a short story with characters sharing the same mental health problem. By the end of the semester, the club will compile the stories into a student-produced psychology magazine/journal, joining creative storytelling with rigorous academic insights.
