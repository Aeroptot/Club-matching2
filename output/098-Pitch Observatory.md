## 社团介绍

Football is far more than a sport—it is a magnifying glass for history, politics, social class, migration, and identity. From El Clásico in Spain to the Superclásico in Argentina, from the Milan Derby to the North West Derby in England, every classic rivalry carries within it decades or even centuries of cultural tension and collective memory. Our ECA is dedicated to unearthing these stories. The Football Culture Studies Club is a student-led organization that aims to go beyond scores and highlights, delving deeply into the historical roots, social conflicts, and fan cultures behind the world's famous derbies. Through weekly topic sharing, guided discussions, and member-led research projects, we seek to understand: why does football mean so much to people across the globe?
