## 社团介绍

SCIE Robotics Team is SCIE’s student-led FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC) programme, returning for its third year. Each season, members work together to design, build, program and drive a robot for a new competition game released worldwide. FTC combines the energy and teamwork of a sport with real engineering, creativity and strategy. The team will compete in official FTC qualifying tournaments, work towards advancement to championship events. No previous robotics experience is required. New members receive structured training and can explore mechanical design, CAD, electronics, software, computer vision, match strategy, media, business and community outreach. Whether you want to machine a mechanism, write autonomous code, secure a sponsor or present to judges, there is a meaningful role for you. SCIE’s team has won an FTC qualifier, captained a finalist alliance, received second place for the Inspire Award and won the Innovate Award; its 2025–26 robot also reached third in the global TeleOp rankings. Beyond competition, members showcase robotics at school events, visit technology companies, deliver STEM activities for younger students and collaborate with other FIRST teams to expand impactful outreach in China and the wider world. Come build ambitious things, learn from failure, compete and have fun with a supportive team. FIRST Impact Report: https://www.firstinspires.org/hubfs/web/about/report/annual_report_2025.pdf
