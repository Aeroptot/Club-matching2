## 社团介绍

EduSpark is a student-led education and social impact ECA that aims to make learning more engaging, accessible, and connected to the wider world. Members explore real-world topics such as culture, history, economics, technology, mathematics, sustainability, and global inequality, and transform what they learn into interactive educational activities. Rather than simply volunteering as tutors, members will work collaboratively to design discussion-based lessons, educational games, workshops, and outreach projects for younger students and communities with fewer opportunities to access diverse perspectives. The ECA is built around four principles: curiosity, critical thinking, global perspective, and student empowerment. Members will develop their own ideas, test activities, receive feedback, and improve them through reflection. Our work will include both on-campus educational activities and community outreach, including the EduSpark Shanxi teaching project. Through these experiences, we hope to create a sustainable cycle: students learn, students design, students teach, and students create positive social impact. Ultimately, EduSpark aims to help both our members and the students we reach see a bigger world, think more deeply, and believe that they can make a difference.
