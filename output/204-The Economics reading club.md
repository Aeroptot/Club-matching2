## 社团介绍

The Economics Reading Club is a student-led enrichment programme designed for our most able and intellectually curious economists, offering a bridge between the A-Level syllabus and the wider world of economic thought. Run by the Economics Department, the club draws on preliminary reading lists recommended by Oxford and Cambridge for Economics applicants, giving students genuine exposure to the kind of texts, arguments, and thinkers they will encounter at university and in admissions interviews.
