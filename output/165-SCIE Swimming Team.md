## 社团介绍

Swimming Team offers an inclusive platform for both enthusiastic recreational swimmers and competitive skilled athletes. Members improve physical fitness, build stamina and develop water‑safety awareness including basic lifesaving knowledge through regular group training sessions. We value teamwork and collective spirit greatly: teammates support one another during practice, share skills and encourage each other to push personal limits. Participants take part in inter‑school swimming competitions, where they gain real‑world competitive experience, learn to handle pressure and foster sportsmanship. Beyond races, our team creates a welcoming community. Regardless of initial ability, every member gets opportunities to grow, cooperate with peers and build resilience both in and out of the pool.
