## 社团介绍

NeighborLink acts as a bridge connecting SCIE students and Volunteer Shenzhen. We curate suitable student volunteer projects, offer clear registration guidance, and build team platforms for participants to join community service together. Members gain official, verifiable volunteer service records. This ECA helps students step outside campus, develop empathy, communication and problem-solving skills, while contributing youth energy to local communities.
