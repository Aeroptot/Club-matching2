## 社团介绍

The is an ECA of students who have high level of skills in a string or woodwind instrument play classical music together. We are a group of students who are dedicated to letting people to appreciate the beauty of classical music through our well-rehearsed performances on stage, including the openings for MUN, TED x SCIE, and Art weeks. This is an opportunity to unleash our emotions during busy school days and feel connected through playing the epic pieces composed by brilliant mind in the past, especially the pieces in the Baroque period and Romantic period. We would be contributing to the school’s vibrancy and variety while advocating the spirit of classical music as our core values.
