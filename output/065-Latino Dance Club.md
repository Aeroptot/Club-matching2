## 社团介绍

The Latino Dance Club aims to gather students passionate about or interested in Latin dance. We will meet weekly to practice moves, learn choreography, and prepare performances for school events. Members will train in a variety of Latin dance styles, including Cha-cha, Rumba, Samba, Paso doble and Jive. This club seeks to foster teamwork, confidence, and a love for dance among members. Whether refining technique by diving deeper into Latin rhythm and movement or discovering a new hobby，members will thrive in an inclusive, lively, and ‘Latino’ environment.
