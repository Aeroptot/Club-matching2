## 社团介绍

Our extracurricular activity group is dedicated to creating a safe, open, and comfortable environment where students can freely exchange their unique insights into 3D modeling and explore its practical applications in historical technology, daily life and society. Beyond sharing technical skills, we strive to foster meaningful dialogue among members, encouraging everyone to draw new inspiration and innovative ideas from each other's experiences. We believe learning is a collaborative process, where through active discussion and mutual support, each participant gains valuable knowledge that transcends the software itself. Furthermore, we are committed to translating collective expertise into concrete action. By building a united and collaborative team, we aim to bridge the gap between modeling theory and real-world engineering challenges. Our ultimate goal is to design and implement innovative solutions that meet societal needs—whether it's assistive devices, sustainable design, or community-centered projects. Through this process, we hope to not only enhance our technical skills but also cultivate social responsibility and teamwork, demonstrating that even small-scale creations can have a profound impact on the world around us.
