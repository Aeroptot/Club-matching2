## 社团介绍

Our society is a student-run academic group focused on mathematics and physics, operating within our school. The core activity is simple: we invite experienced members—senior students who have a solid grasp of particular topics—to give regular walk-through sessions on standard textbooks, helping fellow students navigate difficult derivations, solve problems step by step, and clarify common misconceptions. Unlike formal lectures, our sessions are interactive and discussion-friendly; attendees are encouraged to interrupt with questions whenever something is unclear. We believe that peer-to-peer explanation, coming from someone who recently struggled with the same material, is often the most accessible bridge between a dense textbook and genuine understanding. Beyond our weekly sessions, we maintain a small public account where we share concise reading notes, worked examples, and occasional popular-science pieces. We might also collaborate with the school's sci-fi club on informal joint meetups in the future, where we pick a scientific idea from a novel or film and walk through how plausible it really is under known physics—just for fun and curiosity. Our society is not about grand ambitions; it is about building a friendly, low-pressure space where students who enjoy math and physics can share what they know, help each other learn, and maybe discover that these two subjects are less intimidating when tackled together.
