## 社团介绍

The Theoretical Physics Club is a student-led academic ECA designed to introduce students to fundamental physics that can help consolidate their background knowledge. We will not delve into very complicated topics, and members are only required to know P1 Calculus. Using Physics for Scientists and Engineers (6th Edition) by Paul A. Tipler and Gene Mosca as our main textbook, we explore fundamental concepts through a combination of guided teaching, mathematical derivations, conceptual discussions, and challenging problem-solving. The club will cover major areas of physics including mechanics, oscillations and waves, thermodynamics, electromagnetism, optics, and introductory modern physics. Sessions will be interactive, where members are encouraged to discuss and challenge each other with their own understandings. It will operate more likely as a study group rather than lecture-based classes.
