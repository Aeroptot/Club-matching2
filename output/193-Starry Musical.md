## 社团介绍

Starry Musical aims to provide a platform for discussions between musical lovers and works for the publication of musicals. We welcome every musical lover, or anyone willing to know about musicals. Unlike other musical-related clubs focusing on performing musicals, we mainly create a place for non-singers and non-actors to feel the charm of musicals, which is also a promotion of the thought that “musicals are for everyone as long as you are interested in it”. We will discuss official recordings of musicals, including famous and outstanding foreign ones from Broadway or West End, and more importantly, some Chinese musicals. We want to promote and publicize that there are also good musicals in China, originally produced by Chinese playwrights and Chinese musical actors and actresses. After watching every musical, we plan to showcase our understanding and publicize the musicals by posting videos on social media. Starry Musical channel has already been registered on many platforms online. Everyone in Starry Musical would be responsible for part of the videos, including but not limited to video editing, speakers in the videos and scriptwriters. Also, we plan to link musicals to other topics, like mental illness, depending on the musicals we are watching.
