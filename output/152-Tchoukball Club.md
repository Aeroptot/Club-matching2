## 社团介绍

This is Tchoukball ECA, we come together due to the passion for this amazing sport. You may not have even heard of this, but you will learn it in your core PE classes. This is a sport that originated in Switzerland and is now becoming popular in most Asian countries and Regions. This sport is super fun; you won't have any aggressive body contact; instead, all you need to do is work closely with your teammates, act fast, and try your best strategy and angle to attack the net to get points. If you want to make friends with each other and get really interesting experiences, please come to our ECA. Here you can find the best way that teamwork and strategy work with each other. This year, we have two vice presidents: Ethan 24050 and Vitas 25447.
