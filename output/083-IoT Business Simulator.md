## 社团介绍

We are a student-led club where technology meets entrepreneurship. Members from engineering, business, design, and other backgrounds work together to turn real-world problems into IoT products using ESP32 and other accessible technologies. From identifying a problem and conducting market research to designing, prototyping, testing, and marketing a product on real world platforms, members experience the complete journey from idea to product. In addition, we design activities that directly focus on benefiting your US application, blending ideas of leadership, creativity, and social impacts.
