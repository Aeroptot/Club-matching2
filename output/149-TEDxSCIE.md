## 社团介绍

TEDxSCIE is the a student-led organisation at SCIE. Since 2017, we have run for 9 years and delivered 8 annual conferences, each bringing together many students and teachers. We turn "Ideas Worth Spreading" into practice by creating an open platform for genuine intellectual exchange. Our weekly workshops cover topic brainstorming, speech writing, verbal delivery, body language, and confidence-building. We also use internal speech competitions to help members improve through practice. The team is divided into four functional departments: Academic, Publicity, Planning, and External Relations. Members get hands-on experience in every stage of organising a large-scale event—from theme selection and speaker outreach to stage execution and on-site coordination. Last academic year, we received the Best Leadership ECA award at SCIE.
