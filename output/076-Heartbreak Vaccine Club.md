## 社团介绍

The Heartbreak Vaccine Club is a student organization dedicated to promoting mental health and well-being on campus. Through peer mentoring, we provide a safe, supportive, and non-judgmental space where students can share their struggles with academics, relationships, and everyday life. Our members are trained in basic counseling and active listening skills, fostering empathy and understanding. Beyond one-on-one support, we also run a public platform to share accessible psychology knowledge and practical strategies for emotional resilience. Our mission is to normalize conversations around mental health and to build a campus culture of care, empathy, and connection.
