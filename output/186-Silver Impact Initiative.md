## 社团介绍

Silver Impact Initiative is a student-led club dedicated to improving the lives of older adults through science, community action, and youth leadership. Each month, members explore a real-world challenge affecting older adults, learn from scientific research and experts, and develop practical ideas to address the issue. Not only do we aim to help the elderly, we also reflect upon ourselves after identifying the issues of the elderly to learn how to avoid some preventable issues. Our activities follow a four-step model: Learn → Ideate → Act → Reflect. Students may participate in research, volunteering, community projects, design, or innovation. Members also have opportunities to become Project Leaders, where they develop an idea, form a team, coordinate implementation, and measure the project’s outcomes all with the help of resources of the ECA and its members . Projects remain part of the broader Silver Impact Initiative, allowing future students to build upon previous work and creating continuity beyond individual events. Our initial topics include cancer, Alzheimer’s disease, and hearing loss, with a particular focus on developing a long-term hearing-care initiative for older adults. We aim to connect students with appropriate experts, healthcare institutions, community organizations, and nonprofit or foundation resources where possible.
