## 社团介绍

BioChem Fever is a student-led club designed to ignite curiosity and creativity in the fields of biology and chemistry. Our activities combine hands-on experiments, scientific discussions, and problem-based projects to help members deepen their understanding of the natural sciences beyond the classroom. The club will cover molecular biology, biochemistry, and applications in biotechnology, giving students the opportunity to explore fascinating topics such as enzyme activity, synthetic biology, and environmental applications of biochemistry. By integrating practical experiments with theory, members will gain confidence in experimental design, data analysis, and scientific communication. BioChem Fever aims to be both educational and enjoyable, cultivating teamwork and inspiring future scientists.
