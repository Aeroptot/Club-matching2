## 社团介绍

We are a student-led research collective dedicated to dissecting aviation disasters through the lens of public safety. Every session, we reconstruct one historic crash—from cockpit voice recordings to wreckage distribution—and apply the Swiss Cheese Model to trace how minor errors align into catastrophic outcomes. Beyond storytelling, we build our own database: mapping accident hotspots, analyzing pilot age-to-experience correlation (the “U‑curve” of risk), and studying human factors like crew familiarity and circadian rhythms. Our mission is not morbid curiosity, but rational vigilance—transforming tragedy into actionable safety intelligence. We believe every black box holds a lesson not just for aviation, but for emergency response, risk communication, and everyday decision-making. If you seek truth in data and respect in remembrance, join us in rewriting the odds—because the safest flight is the one we never stop learning from.
