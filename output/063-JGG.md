## 社团介绍

JGG is a music club built on joy, energy, and good vibes. The name comes from the Chinese “Jiao Gao Gao,” meaning “standing on tiptoes and reaching higher.” It also stands for “Just Get Groovy.” We are a group of music lovers who enjoy playing, creating, and jamming together. Our style blends rock, pop, and indie influences. We rehearse regularly, write original songs, and perform at campus and city events. We believe making music should be fun, not stressful. Whether you play an instrument or just love to sing and listen, everyone is welcome. Join JGG, get groovy, and turn the volume up!
