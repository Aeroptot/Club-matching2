## 社团介绍

DECODE THE WORLD is an interdisciplinary and project-based ECA that explores how mathematics, data, computer science, and scientific thinking can be used to understand and solve real-world problems. Each year we decode a different domain by breaking complex problems into measurable components to find patterns in data and building computational solutions. This year's theme is DECODE SOUND, exploring the science and technology behind sound through signal processing, Fourier analysis, spectrograms, algorithms, and AI. At the end of every session there is a short technical lesson with a practical experiment or mini-build, allowing members to immediately apply what they learn. No prior coding (we will be doing vibe coding) or music experience is required. The goal to learn how to apply knowledge from different disciplines to problems beyond the classroom
