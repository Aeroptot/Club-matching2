## 社团介绍

Mathematical Structures Society (MSS) is a student-led mathematics exploration community that aims to help students discover the deeper ideas behind mathematics beyond the standard curriculum. Rather than focusing only on competition training or solving individual problems, MSS encourages students to explore how mathematical ideas are connected through patterns, structures, and logical reasoning. Through workshops, discussions, problem-solving sessions, and student presentations, members will gradually develop the ability to move from concrete problems toward abstract mathematical thinking. The club will introduce accessible topics from areas such as mathematical reasoning, combinatorics, number theory, symmetry, graph theory, cryptography, and introductory abstract algebra. By connecting mathematics with real-world applications, art, technology, and everyday phenomena, MSS hopes to make advanced mathematical thinking approachable for high school students.
