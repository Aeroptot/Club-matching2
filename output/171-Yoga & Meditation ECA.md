## 社团介绍

The Yoga and Meditation Club aims to promote physical wellness, mental clarity, and overall well-being. We offer a welcoming space for students to explore yoga and meditation, with weekly sessions focused on yoga asanas (stretching and strength), meditation practices, and sound therapy using various instruments. We also share knowledge on key techniques and terminology to deepen our practice.
