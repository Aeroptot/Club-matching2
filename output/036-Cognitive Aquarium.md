## 社团介绍

Cognitive Aquarium is an experiment-based cognitive science and behavioural science club that requires no prerequisite in psychology background. We will give an intro about what is cognitive science and specify its comprising blocks and components. Participants will ideally gain skills on reading, writing, interpreting and summarising research studies throughout hands-on experiment process. Each typical session will be a deep dive into a specific cognitive science concepts—e.g. memory, decision-making, attention, perception, etc— and usually includes 1. A short lecture/explanation 2. An activity (such as a game, a social interaction game, or other interactive tasks) 3. Replication of an classical or fun experiment (with participants trying to summarise the results) 4. A post-class assignment to read a relevant paper, discuss interesting ideas or "fun facts" gleaned from it, and log them in a shared document.
