Our club Paper Airplane Technology Institute (will be called as ‘PATI’ in the following) is a club that is aimed to teach and support our members to learn, make and fly their paper airplane made by themselves. We try to combine aviation principles, physics and handcrafting as interests into the events of our club in the following semester.
