## 社团介绍

Constellation Lab is an interdisciplinary academic ECA that connects students with competitions, research opportunities and collaborative projects across business, STEM, social sciences, writing and community impact. Members will discover and evaluate opportunities, organise reliable information, and form teams based on complementary skills, interests, availability and commitment. The ECA will also develop and test CoQuest, a WeChat Mini Program that provides a verified opportunity calendar and a safe, structured team-matching process. Members may contribute through research, programming, product design, writing, data organisation, communication or project leadership. No previous competition or technical experience is required. The ECA welcomes students who are curious, responsible and willing to turn ideas and opportunities into meaningful projects.
