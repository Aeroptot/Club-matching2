## 社团介绍

Fanart Club is a place where students can make art and share their love for movies, books, games, and other fictional stories. We want to build a friendly space where people with similar interests can talk freely about their favorite characters and worlds. During ECA time, we work together to come up with simple and fun ideas for art, as well as give members prompts and suggestions to help them get started. Students don't need to be a great artist to join, any beginners are welcome too. The club is about having fun, being creative, and meeting new friends who like the same things you do. We also share tips and support each other.
