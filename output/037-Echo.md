## 社团介绍

This ECA combines introductory social‑psychological knowledge with light‑hearted recreational activities to promote accessible mental‑well‑being awareness among students. We deliver beginner‑friendly psychology tips covering emotion regulation, stress management and self‑acceptance, avoiding overly complex academic jargon. Most sessions prioritize entertaining interactive segments, including casual group discussions, mini games and scenario‑sharing, instead of rigid lectures. Members will learn practical, daily‑usable mental‑health skills to recognize personal emotional signals and build healthier mindsets. This activity creates a safe, relaxed peer space, where participants can reduce psychological pressure, understand simple social‑psychological logic, and support one another’s mental wellness in an enjoyable atmosphere.
