## 社团介绍

Psychology Union is a proposed ECA open to all SCIE students. It will tentatively be held every Monday as a weekly lecture-based activity. The main aim is to bring together psychology-related clubs at SCIE and create a shared platform for students who are interested in psychology. Instead of functioning as one single club, Psychology Union will work more like a network. Students can attend weekly lectures to gain a general understanding of psychology, and they can also join different club projects based on their own interests.
