## 社团介绍

This Paper Quilling ECA is a creative art community dedicated to the traditional craft of paper quilling — the art of rolling, shaping and gluing thin paper strips to form delicate decorative artworks. We welcome students of all skill levels, from complete beginners to those with prior crafting experience. In our sessions, members will master fundamental quilling techniques and create a wide range of works, including bookmarks, greeting cards, wall art and 3D miniatures. Beyond hands-on creation, the club integrates creative expression, aesthetic appreciation and public welfare. We will organise themed creation projects and donate selected works to community charity programs. Our club aims to provide a relaxing, supportive space for students to unwind from academic pressure, nurture patience and fine motor skills, and connect with peers who share a passion for handmade art.
