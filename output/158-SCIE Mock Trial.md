## 社团介绍

SCIE Mock trial will train students to become skilled attorneys and witnesses, prepared to participate in high level mock trial competitions such as International Mock Trial, Peer Potential Mock Trial, etc. Through teaching the federal rules of evidence, procedures of a trial, public speaking skills, performance tips, students will be prepared for success in mock trial while also getting an introduction to law related knowledge.
