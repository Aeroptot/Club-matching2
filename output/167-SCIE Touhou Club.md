## 社团介绍

SCIE Touhou Club will become a creative and cultural hub dedicated to exploring the rich, fan-driven ecosystem of the Touhou Project—one of the world's most iconic indie subcultures, which includes vibrant secondary creations, spanning diverse music remixes, digital art, comics, fanbooks, literature, and game design. This club offers an enviroment for students to appreciate unique soundtracks, analyze East Asian folklore/mythology, and share creative works.
