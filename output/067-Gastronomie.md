## 社团介绍

This ECA is an interdisciplinary club integrating food history, food politics, cultural anthropology and practical culinary techniques. It moves beyond casual food‑tasting activities. Members explore how ingredients travelled across empires, how food reflects power structures, social stratification and global trade. We also cover classical cooking fundamentals alongside avant‑garde modernist cuisine including molecular gastronomy.
