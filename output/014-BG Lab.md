## 社团介绍

BG Lab is a student-led ECA that introduces members to a wide range of board game genres, mechanics, and design concepts through both gameplay and analysis. Members will have the opportunity to design and develop their own original board games from scratch, progressing from an initial idea to rules, prototypes, playtesting, and a finished product. In addition, BG Lab aims to connect members with experienced board game designers and visual artists, giving them insight into the professional board game industry and, where possible, opportunities to collaborate with professionals to further develop their own projects.
