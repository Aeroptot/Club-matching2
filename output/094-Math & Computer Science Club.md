## 社团介绍

Math & Computer Science Club focuses on algorithms, programming, and mathematical thinking beyond the school curriculum. Members will explore classic algorithms, data structures, computational problems, and their mathematical foundations through explanations, coding, and group discussion. The club is open to students with different levels of programming experience.
