## 社团介绍

Introduce students to traditional Chinese fitness and meditation through basic Baduanjin, Tai Chi, stretching, breathing and mindfulness exercises. No previous experience is required. Sessions will focus on movement, balance, flexibility, concentration and relaxation, while also exploring the cultural background of these traditional practices.
