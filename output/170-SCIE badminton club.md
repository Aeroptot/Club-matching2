## 社团介绍

School Badminton Club Our school badminton club is a friendly and energetic place for students who enjoy playing badminton and want to improve their skills. The club welcomes both beginners and experienced players, so everyone has a chance to participate. During weekly training sessions, members practise important skills such as serving, smashing, footwork, and teamwork. Coaches also organise fitness exercises and practice matches to help students become stronger and more confident on the court. Besides regular training, the club takes part in different competitions throughout the school year. Members can compete in singles and doubles matches against with each others. These events encourage students to challenge themselves, learn from their mistakes, and improve their performance. Overall, the badminton club is a great opportunity to stay active, make new friends, develop sporting skills, and enjoy exciting competitions.
