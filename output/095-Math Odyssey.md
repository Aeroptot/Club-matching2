## 社团介绍

Math Odyssey focus on math competition tutoring and discussion. Our ECA is devoted to creating a professional and adaptable environment for students who are interested in math and not satified by the school curriculum and students who potentially want to apply math-related major in future. We will systematically teach topics in math contests. Our weekly course introduces Algebra, Combination, Geometry etc and past paper questions from contests. We will focus on some mainstream contests like AMC, AIME, BMO and occasionally included some advanced maths topics such as abstract algebra, and almost anything you want to learn about maths! Except for the weekly lessons, the club will also provide a platform for members to discuss intriguing problems, share creative solutions and find like-minded peers.
