## 社团介绍

SEG, Science Education for Girls, started in 2020, is open to all who share a love for exploration. We aim to advocate for female diseases and mental health illnesses. We have been posting essays on multiple social media sources, including WeChat accounts and Red Notes. We are committed to encouraging more girls to emphasize not only the sciences, but also their health. To pursue dreams with support and guidance. This year, we will create a space where female mental illnesses are emphasized, creating an opportunity for members to explore topics from pre-menstrual syndrome to Briquet’s syndrome. Members can enjoy discussions; together, we can make an impact in our society.
