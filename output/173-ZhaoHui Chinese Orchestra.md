## 社团介绍

Our ECA brings together students who share a passion for playing traditional Chinese instruments in an ensemble. Based on the solid groundwork of members from previous years, our ECA now consists of wind, string, and percussion sections. By adapting popular music or rehearsing classical pieces, we introduce modern aesthetics that interact with traditional performance practices. During ECA, we practice solo and ensemble repertoire. We cherish our rehearsal time and actively participate in campus competitions, willing to share our appreciation of Chinese traditional music with all schoolmates.
