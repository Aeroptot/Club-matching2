## 社团介绍

This is a formal 5v5 basketball tournament in SCIE. There are 5 teams in this ECA lead by various captains-all are from SISAC school team or ISL school team-to compete for the final championship in the end of this school year. There will be a tryount monitored by coaches from SISAC and ISL team to ensure the impartiality of picking the players. Every basketball enthusiasts can attend this ECA. The players are drafted by five teams' captains to form a team that at least contain 8 team members. The rountine in the ECA are divided into two parts-regular season and the playoffs season. Firstly, the five teams are playing against each other during the regular season to get the four playoffs spot, meaning that one team will not be in the playoffs competitions. Then, the games in playoffs will be one-off games, which makes winning more coincidential and ensures the tournament will end before the march(won't affect by the external examination schedule).
