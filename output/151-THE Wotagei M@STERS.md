## 社团介绍

The club focuses on WotaGei, a dynamic Japanese pop dance born out of the glowsticks moves originally for fans to express the support to the people they like on stages. The dancer usually holds two high-brightness glowsticks, and perform intensely, leaving beautiful trails of light in the air. The study of OtaGei, the varient of WotaGei will also be involved in the club activities. Unlike WotaGei, OtaGei uses no glowsticks, and is typically not considered as stage performances. Instead, it is more of a relax meathod for the dancer to immerse and enjoy the background music. For the estimated 20 years since WotaGei has been founded, numerous enthusiasts have been creating new Wotagei choreography, also known as Waza. There will always be new things to learn and perfect in this unique foreign dance.
