## 社团介绍

Olympiad in Informatics is a competitive programming activity of algorithms and discrete mathematics. Club members solve selected, inspiring problems together on online judge platforms (e.g. Codeforces, Luogu); specific problems are chosen to best reveal how intriguing the subject is. The club also focuses on highlighting the fascinating intersection between mathematics and computer science, for example how computers could aid counting problems and logic verification. Above all, our primary goal is to share how engaging and fun competitive programming can be, gathering more people to join. We will also organize students to participate in online/offline competitive programming contests in allocated teams.
