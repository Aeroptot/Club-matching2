## 社团介绍

"Nice Calculus" is the newly rebranded continuation of our previous "Calculus" ECA. Derived from the names of its co-leaders, Niko and Bruce, the name "Nice" reflects our club's core educational philosophy. This year, we go beyond teaching rigorous calculus concepts and advanced techniques; our primary focus is helping students develop a profound mathematical intuition—a crucial skill often overlooked in traditional high school curriculums. We will achieve this through a highly visual teaching style, utilizing custom-programmed mathematics animations (such as those built with Python's Manim) and interactive visualization applications. By stepping away from purely algebraic problem-solving, we aim to demonstrate that sensing math geometrically, physically, and visually strengthens independent thinking and fundamentally transforms how students understand the nature of mathematics. Furthermore, based on feedback from previous terms, we have significantly upgraded our syllabus. We are extending our lessons to include applied mathematics topics, such as the multivariable calculus essential for higher-level physics and engineering. By pairing these advanced concepts with concrete examples and visual tools, we ensure our members gain a comprehensive, well-rounded mastery of mathematics.
