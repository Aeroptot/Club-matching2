## 社团介绍

Fitness ECA aims to help students develop a scientific, safe, and sustainable approach to resistance training and physical fitness. Rather than simply exercising together, members will learn the principles behind effective training, including exercise technique, movement mechanics, training progression, recovery, and basic program design. As the ECA leader, I have 3 years of self-weight training and 2 years of systematic resistance-training experience and have developed a deeper understanding of exercise selection, movement patterns, training volume and intensity, fatigue management, and the logic behind different training approaches. I hope to share these ideas in an accessible and practical way, helping members understand not only how to perform an exercise, but also why it is performed in a certain way. Sessions will combine short explanations, practical demonstrations, movement workshops, form analysis, and discussion. The ECA will be suitable for both beginners who want to start training safely and more experienced students who want to improve their understanding of training.
