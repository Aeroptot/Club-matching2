## 社团介绍

The Mosaic DIY ECA is a hands-on creative arts club where students explore the ancient craft of mosaic-making. We design and assemble colourful patterns and images using tiles, glass, beads, and recycled materials. We also study the cultural history of mosaics across civilisations, linking art with heritage. Our ECA is a safe, inclusive space for experimentation, self-expression, and mindful making.
