## 社团介绍

SCIE Singing Club is a place for students who enjoy singing or want to improve their singing skills. The club focuses on teaching basic singing techniques, such as vocal warm-ups, breathing control, pitch, and rhythm. We hope to create a comfortable and supportive environment where everyone can practice singing without feeling nervous or pressured. The club welcomes students of all experience levels, including beginners who may not have much confidence in their voice. Through regular practices and group activities, members can gradually improve their skills and become more comfortable performing in front of others. If possible, we hope to participate in school performances such as SGT, giving members opportunities to share their progress and experience singing on stage.
