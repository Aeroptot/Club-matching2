## 社团介绍

HugS(Helping Unique Growing Souls) is determined to provide help for the children with physical disabilities by multiple methods, for example, advocacy, education, and hands-on volunteer activities. Children with disabilities include those with physical disabilities, intellectual or cognitive disorders, and neurodevelopmental disorders such as autism spectrum disorder. Although awareness of providing special education to those children has improved in recent years, the phenomenon of a lack of public understanding, social stigma, and limited opportunities for accessing inclusive education persists. As a result, the primary aim of our club would be to improve their situation in society in the long term and provide common knowledge of them to those with limited understanding. By collaborating with local welfare centers, we want to provide more meaningful social engagement for students and the community so that they are able to engage with these special children and understand their special situation and lived experiences. Members are also encouraged to participate in awareness campaigns and the promotion of special education.
