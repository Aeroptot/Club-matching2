## 社团介绍

We are a student-led community dedicated to empowering young people to find their voice, build confidence, and think critically about the world around them threw off-campus seminars and open discussions. Our goal is not to give answers, but to create a safe space where teenagers can ask bold questions, challenge stereotypes, and support one another in becoming more thoughtful, independent individuals. We also aim to promote gender equality within campus, creating a safe and welcoming environment for students. We’ll also have an exhibition to showcase our work in the end.
