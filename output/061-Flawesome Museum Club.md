## 社团介绍

This ECA uses art and storytelling to explore the imperfections, challenges, and unexpected moments that shape people’s lives. This year, its main focus will be elderly care and the experiences of older adults, using creative expression to encourage students to look beyond stereotypes of aging and understand the people behind them. Students will visit elderly-care communities and related facilities, where they will observe daily life, conduct interviews, and explore topics such as aging, memories, family relationships, loneliness, independence, dignity, and the changing relationship between generations. They will work together to create a documentary that records these experiences and gives elderly participants an opportunity to share their own stories and perspectives. Alongside documentary filmmaking, students will transform what they learn into different forms of art, including photography, painting, writing, illustration, and installation. These works will explore themes such as memory, aging, vulnerability, resilience, and human connection. The creative process will encourage students to reflect on their own assumptions about aging while developing empathy, communication, and storytelling skills. The ECA will culminate in an exhibition combining student artwork, documentary footage, photographs, and other materials collected throughout the project. By bringing these stories to a wider audience, the exhibition will encourage conversations about elderly care and intergenerational understanding, ultimately turning individual stories and everyday experiences into meaningful narratives of connection.
