## 社团介绍

The iGEM Club is a student-led extracurricular activity dedicated to synthetic biology, biotechnology, and interdisciplinary problem-solving. As part of the global iGEM (International Genetically Engineered Machine) competition framework, the club will design, build, and test innovative biological systems to address real-world challenges. Activities include laboratory training, project design, outreach initiatives, and collaborations with international teams. Members will gain experience in scientific research, teamwork, and communication while exploring the ethical and societal dimensions of biotechnology. The club fosters creativity and scientific curiosity, offering students an opportunity to engage with cutting-edge science in a collaborative and supportive environment.
