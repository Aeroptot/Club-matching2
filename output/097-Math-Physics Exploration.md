## 社团介绍

Physics–Math Exploration is a student-led academic club for students who would like to explore how mathematical ideas become tools for understanding physical systems. Starting from familiar classroom topics such as motion, forces, waves and energy, each session will begin with a modelling question(near our learning curriculum): What should we represent? Which assumptions are reasonable? What can the mathematics predict, and where does the model stop working? Sessions will combine short explanations(trying with both languages), guided discussion, and one or two carefully chosen problems(for each session). Useful mathematical tools—including vectors, trigonometry, parameterisation, graphs, introductory calculus ideas and data analysis—will be introduced when they become useful, rather than as isolated techniques. The aim is not simply to move ahead through a syllabus, but to develop curiosity, precise reasoning and confidence in turning everyday questions into solvable physical models. Students with different levels of mathematical experience are welcome; optional extension problems and small projects will provide further challenge.
