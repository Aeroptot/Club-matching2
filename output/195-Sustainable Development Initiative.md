## 社团介绍

Sustainable development initiative has been running for three years. Our hope and mission is to find sustainable ways of living and developing by experimenting in our school and advocate for attention. Our projects include the sustainable garden, which uses zero-chemicals in the growing, and the compost plan, which collaborates with the GC department to make organic compost from kitchen wastes and fallen leaves.
