## 社团介绍

Ever wondered why a Formula 1 car sticks to the ground, how aircraft wings generate lift, or how engineers test a design before it is ever built? In this ECA, you will turn your own ideas into 3D models using Rhino, then bring them into STAR-CCM+ to see how air, fluids, heat and other physical forces interact with your designs. Instead of simply learning software, you will work like an engineer: design, simulate, analyze, improve, and test again. Projects may include aerodynamic cars, aircraft wings, drones, turbines, cooling systems, or even designs of your own choice. No previous experience with 3D modelling or simulation is required—we will start from the basics and gradually move toward more advanced projects. If you enjoy engineering, physics, design, cars, aviation, or simply want to see whether your ideas actually work in the real world, this ECA gives you a chance to explore engineering beyond the classroom.
