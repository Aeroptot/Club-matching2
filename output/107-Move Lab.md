## 社团介绍

MOVE Lab is a student-led club focused on sports science, training, and physical performance. The club aims to make scientific knowledge about exercise and the human body accessible and useful to students of all experience levels, from complete beginners to regular athletes. Sessions will focus on explaining the reasoning behind training methods rather than simply giving students routines to follow.
