## 社团介绍

The AnthnoPulse Lab is an anthropology-focused club dedicated to uncovering the hidden meanings in everyday life. We treat our school and city as living field sites, investigating how objects, spaces, and routines reflect human behavior and social change. Unlike traditional academic clubs, we don’t just read about culture—we go out to observe it. Through systematic fieldwork, interviews, and creative documentation, we explore questions like: How do students personalize their dorm spaces? How do different generations use the same public park? What do market transactions reveal about community values? Each semester, members collaborate on a micro-ethnography project, producing field notes, interview excerpts, and visual responses. The AnthnoPulse club is a space for curious minds to develop observational rigor, cultural empathy, and a nuanced understanding of the diverse communities we inhabit.
