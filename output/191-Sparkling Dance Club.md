## 社团介绍

Sparkling is the ideal platform for students who love dancing and aspire to grow as confident performers. Through structured weekly practices and performance preparations, the club helps members refine their dancing skills and gain stage experience at major school events like the SGT, NYC, Halloween, and Fashion Show, immersing themselves in the school’s lively culture. Additionally, the club organizes activities such as random dance sessions during Senior Week, spreading the joy of dance to the wider school community.
