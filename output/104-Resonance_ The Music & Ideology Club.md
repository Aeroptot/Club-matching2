## 社团介绍

Resonance is a discussion-based ECA dedicated to exploring the deeper ideologies, political messages, and social representations embedded within popular and alternative music. Far from just listening to catchy tunes, we analyze music as a historical and cultural artifact. We will dissect how artists use their platforms to express the struggles of specific demographics, challenge the status quo, and reflect the historical zeitgeist of their eras. For example, we will analyze Green Day's American Idiot as a critique of post-9/11 media paranoia, exploring who the "idiot" represents and what generational anxieties the album voices. By bridging music with social sciences and history, members will develop critical media literacy, learn to interpret complex narratives, and enjoy lively, respectful debates about the soundtracks that shape our society.
