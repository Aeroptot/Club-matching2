## 社团介绍

AI Ethics & Governance Roundtable is a source-based discussion and writing ECA for students interested in how artificial intelligence should be developed, regulated and used. Each meeting examines a concrete policy question through primary materials such as legislation, regulatory guidance, institutional frameworks, research papers and real cases. Topics may include risk-based AI regulation, transparency and labelling of AI-generated content, copyright and training data, algorithmic bias, biometric surveillance, education, open-source models, frontier-model safety and responsibility for automated decisions. Members will distinguish facts, legal requirements, ethical principles and stakeholder interests before joining a moderated roundtable. They will then write short position memos that state a claim, address counterarguments and propose an actionable policy. No prior legal or technical knowledge is required. The aim is not to reach one approved opinion, but to develop evidence-based judgment, careful disagreement and responsible public reasoning. By the end of the semester, members will produce a reviewed collection of position papers and present selected arguments in a public AI governance forum.
