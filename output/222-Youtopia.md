## 社团介绍

Our ECA is an original economic card game creation society built on a self-designed fictional continent with diverse unique nations. In previous years, our core focus was fusing formal economic academic knowledge with tabletop gameplay, as our team independently designed, balanced and refined a proprietary card game that models real-world market cycles, trade policies and resource allocation. For the upcoming academic year, we pursue dual-track progress: we continue iterating new card expansions and gameplay mechanics, while vastly expanding our lore framework—crafting core protagonists, layered character backstories, inter-nation conflicts and complete narrative arcs to deepen our immersive fantasy world. Additionally, we will launch official social media channels to consistently publish serialized worldbuilding entries, character biographies, game tutorials and behind-the-scenes development content, letting members collectively build a cohesive, self-contained original game IP.
