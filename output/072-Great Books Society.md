## 社团介绍

Great Books Society is a student-led reading and discussion club for students interested in philosophy, literature, history, politics, and the fundamental questions of human life. Each week, members will read a short text or selected excerpts from an influential work and discuss a central question raised by it: What is justice? What makes a good life? Are we truly free? What do we owe to others? What makes political authority legitimate? Possible readings will include works by Plato, Aristotle, Confucius, Shakespeare, John Stuart Mill, Nietzsche, Hannah Arendt, and other major thinkers and writers from different intellectual traditions. No previous knowledge of philosophy is required, and readings will be kept manageable and accessible. Meetings will combine close reading, guided discussion, short debates, thought experiments, and student-led presentations. Rather than looking for one “correct” interpretation, members will learn to question assumptions, examine arguments, listen carefully to different perspectives, and develop their own reasoned responses to enduring human questions.
