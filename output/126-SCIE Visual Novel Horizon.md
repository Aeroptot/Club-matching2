## 社团介绍

SCIE Visual Novel Horizon is an interdisciplinary platform dedicated to exploring visual novels as a dynamic medium uniting interactive literature, visual arts, and digital technology. Guided by our motto, "Visual novels, tech, and explorations beyond the horizon," the club examines the thematic and structural depth of visual novels through multi-faceted critical frameworks, drawing insights from literature, philosophy, psychology, sociology, and ethics. In parallel with literary critique, we deconstruct the technical and creative craftsmanship required to build compelling visual novels. Members share insights into game engine programming, branching narrative structures, artistic styling, and adaptive sound design. SCIE Visual Novel Horizon provides an open, intellectually rigorous stage where students can present their analyses, share developmental techniques, and engage in candid peer debate. By embracing differing viewpoints, agreement, and constructive disagreement, the club cultivates both sharp critical thinkers and capable multimedia creators.
