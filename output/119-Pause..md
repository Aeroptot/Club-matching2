## 社团介绍

‘Pause.’ is a community designed to make mindfulness practical, accessible, and relevant to everyday student life. Rather than treating mindfulness as simply “sitting still and meditating,” the club explores how attention, awareness, and intentional reflection can influence the way we study, communicate, manage stress, and experience everyday moments. Each session combines a short guided mindfulness practice with an interactive activity or discussion. Students may explore breathing and grounding exercises, mindful movement, attention training, gratitude, mindful listening, digital awareness, and reflection. No previous experience is required, and students are encouraged to participate at their own comfort level. The goal is not to eliminate stress or constantly “feel positive,” but to develop the ability to notice our thoughts, emotions, and surroundings without immediately reacting to them. Through regular practice, Pause. aims to help students become more attentive to themselves and the people around them.
