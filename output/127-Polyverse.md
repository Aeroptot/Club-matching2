## 社团介绍

Polyverse is a 3D modelling and rendering club where students are able to explore computer graphics mainly through the app Blender. The members will learn the skills of 3D modelling, texturing, lighting, and rendering by developing through hands on practice. The club aims to provide a creative space where students are able to turn ideas into digital works, such as designing characters and environments to create complete animations. They will collaborate on group projects, share techniques, and explore art and technology. No prior experience is required, and everyone is welcome to learn, create, and experiment.
