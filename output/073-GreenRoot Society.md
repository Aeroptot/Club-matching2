## 社团介绍

GreenRoot Society is a student-led community dedicated to exploring the connections between environmental change and human health—starting with soil. Our journey begins with personal story from Fujian, where agricultural pollution have harmed local farmland, showing the hidden urgency of soil protection. Members will then participate in the Out of Eden Learn – Introduction to Planetary Health learning journey, inspired by Paul Salopek’s slow journalism walk around the world. Through four “Footsteps,” we will: 1. Observe – Take mindful walks in our communities to notice and record environmental and health-related changes. 2. Connect – Build concept maps linking environmental changes such as pollution or biodiversity loss with specific human health impacts. 3. Investigate – Focus on one planetary health issue, research it through observation, interviews, and reliable resources, and share findings. 4. Act – Identify daily choices that affect planetary health and create advocacy resources to inspire change. Through GreenRoot Society, students will combine observation, scientific thinking, and storytelling to become thoughtful, informed advocates for the planet—proving that local actions can contribute to global health.
