## 社团介绍

This student-led Cantonese Linguistics club focuses on the academic study of Cantonese, a vital regional language with unique phonetics, grammar and cultural value. As a native Cantonese speaker, I will lead members to explore its linguistic features, compare it with Mandarin and other Chinese dialects, discuss tone systems, vocabulary evolution and local cultural language phenomena.
