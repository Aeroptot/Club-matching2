## 社团介绍

We are SHUTTER STUDIO, which is a traditional student photography and video ECA established long time ago since 2022-2023. And we are also the first video/photography studio created in SCIE. And we have many achievement in the history, for example, we used to film 2023-2024 SGT semi-final Video, 2023-2024 fashion show video, 2023-2024 world book week video as well. Besides, we are also adept at taking photo, for instance, we used to held many photography booths at Halloween Carnival and also took part in many school activities like SGT, NYC and even Sports Day. And we finally yield many fascinating photos too. Overall, despite we have an extraordinary history in the past, we have to re-start this year due to one year suspension before. However, I think we will still be ambitious, trying our best to capture more photos and cultivate students photography skills and interests this year in the campus. And me, as a new leader of this club, I believe we gonna motivate the development of media and photography in the school this year too.
