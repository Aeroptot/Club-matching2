## 社团介绍

Humanities and Female Lens are two key words for this ECA. Humanities play a crucial role in our lives, especially in the world we now live in. Without literature, history, or philosophy, STEM would become lifeless numbers. Therefore, recognizing the importance of the humanities and how it influences our world is essential for personal and societal growth. In order to understand humanities, we have to approach it with different perspectives. Although there are several ECAs that focus on humanities, this ECA stands out as unique because it offers knowledge through a new, female perspective. Due to gender inequality, many influential women have been overlooked, and their contributions often went unrecognised. ‘Hermanity’ aims to help students appreciate the value of the humanities through a female lens and foster students’ critical thinking, collaboration, and public speaking skills. We thrive to reverse the stereotypes linked with humanities and overcome difficulties in reading and writing. Overall, we aim to foster student’s intrinsic motivations by giving members the opportunities to learn, and offer them a new lens when viewing our world.
