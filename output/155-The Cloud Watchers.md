## 社团介绍

The Cloud Watcher is a scientific observation and study group focused on the atmospheric phenomenon. Members learn to identify and classify clouds and optical phenomena (e.g., halos, rainbows), understand their formation principles through atmospheric physics and meteorology, and explore related wider topics such as front, air mass, storm and terrestrial effects. Activities combine outdoor sky observation, data analysis, and group discussion, fostering both scientific inquiry and a deeper connection with the natural environment.
