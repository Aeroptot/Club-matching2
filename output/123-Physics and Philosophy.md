## 社团介绍

Physics and Philosophy ECA is a student-led interdisciplinary club exploring the conceptual foundations behind scientific theories and their implications for humanity. While physics provides models to describe the universe, philosophy helps us examine the assumptions, interpretations, and consequences behind those models. The club will explore topics including the interpretation of quantum mechanics (such as the measurement problem and Schrödinger’s Cat), the nature of space and time in Einstein’s relativity, the philosophical foundations of scientific methods, and ethical questions arising from technologies such as artificial intelligence and genetic engineering. Activities will include guided discussions, student presentations, thought experiments, debates, and analysis of scientific papers or philosophical texts. Members will be encouraged to move beyond memorising theories and instead ask deeper questions: Why do scientific models work? What does it mean for a theory to represent reality? How should society respond to new technologies? The ECA welcomes students interested in physics, philosophy, mathematics, humanities, or simply exploring challenging ideas through logical reasoning and open discussion.
