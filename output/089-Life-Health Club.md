## 社团介绍

There is no prior competition experience is required—students in grades 9–11 who are passionate about biology, public health, or social service are all welcome! We prioritize hands-on practice over lectures, applying classroom knowledge to real-world problems—focusing on aging populations, home environmental hygiene, mental and physical health among seniors, child left-behind health, and youth public health. You’ll experience the full project lifecycle: from research design and fieldwork to community education. You’ll build authentic, application-ready materials, portfolios, and research outcomes for U.S. college admissions. The club offers diverse roles that support academic profiles—whether you excel at lab work, writing, interviews, or data analysis, there’s a place for you.
