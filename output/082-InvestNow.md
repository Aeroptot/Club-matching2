## 社团介绍

InvestNow aims to make finance accessible, practical, and intellectually engaging, especially for students with little or no prior experience. Through interactive lessons, real-world cases, portfolio simulations, and discussions of current financial news, members will gradually develop the ability to understand businesses, interpret basic financial information, evaluate risk and return, and form their own investment opinions. The semester will culminate in a stock pitch, where students apply what they have learned to research, analyze, and defend an investment idea. Following feedback from previous members, we have redesigned our curriculum to provide a much stronger foundation before introducing advanced material. In addition, our experienced academic team, with strong results in national and international economics and investment competitions, also offers optional advanced sessions covering topics such as DCF valuation, portfolio and risk modelling, and derivatives. Beyond weekly teaching, InvestNow has already developed into a wider platform for financial education and student collaboration. We have organized two inter-school investment competitions involving students from schools spanning Suzhou to Shenzhen, collaborated with the Global Citizenship team to present social-enterprise projects during Sustainability Week, and have been invited to serve as one of the organizing societies for a seven-school Greater Bay Area investment competition.
