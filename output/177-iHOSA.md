## 社团介绍

ECA description 200w Our iHOSA Club is a student-and-teacher-led organization dedicated to exploring biology, psychology, and health wellbeing through academic challenges, community service, and leadership development. Aligned with iHOSA, we focus on three aspects: 1. Dominate iHOSA competitions. Master subjects like Biology, Behavioral Health, and Pathophysiology to conquer the ATC challenges. 2. Create Health Campaigns. Design powerful PSAs and workshops to drive real change in our community, meanwhile expanding this ECA’s worth to the greater society through social media. 3. Learn from the best. We host iHOSA veterans to share their winning strategies and inspire the next generation of health science innovators. Through both lectures and hands-on promotions, we foster scientific curiosity, teamwork, and real-world impact.
