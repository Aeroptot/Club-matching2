## 社团介绍

The SCIE Rhythm Games ECA targets players interested in further understanding rhythm games and making friends with similar interests. The ECA focuses on building strong connections between players and educating players on rhythm game chart production. During each session, a designated presenter will provide a short introduction lasting 15 to 30 minutes around a typical type of rhythm game, famous rhythm game music producer, or chart production. Advanced players knowledgeable about the specific topics could volunteer to be the presenter in charge of the introductions for the weeks, which helps improve the quality of content and sense of participation. Members can stay after the introduction to attend activities such as video recording or join multi-player mode with other members. Regular activities such as song producer guessing, draw and guess, and friendly competition amongst club members will also be available. This ECA plans to provide lessons and activities that alternate between different options. The session will last from 4:45 to 5:15. The Game introduction will focus on a famous rhythm game, introducing its mechanism, style of music and art, well-known songs and characters, and strengths and weaknesses. Music producers' introductions will focus on their music groups, how their style and genre changed over time, their best songs, and the famous rhythm game songs they produced. The presenter will present the topic each week with a PowerPoint presentation and short clips of the game and music. Below is a brief draft for the first 10 ECA sessions.
