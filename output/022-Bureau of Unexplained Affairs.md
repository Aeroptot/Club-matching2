## 社团介绍

This ECA is a civil‑paranormal‑phenomenon research society. We explore reported anomalous cases, including UFO sightings, cryptids such as the Loch Ness Monster, and historical supernatural folklore. Rather than confirming whether paranormal events are real, our core goal is to analyse these accounts from historical, psychological and sociological perspectives. We will study classic materials such as 《Soulstealers: The Chinese Sorcery Scare of 1768》, comparing mass panics in history with modern‑day viral supernatural rumours. Members practise critical‑thinking, source‑checking and group discussion. We evaluate eyewitness reports, media narratives and social‑psychological causes behind collective belief.
