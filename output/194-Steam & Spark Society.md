## 社团介绍

The FTC Robotics ECA is a student-led team that designs, builds, and tests a robot for the FIRST Tech Challenge. Members will work together throughout the engineering process, from understanding the competition requirements and developing initial ideas to creating CAD models, manufacturing parts, assembling mechanisms, and testing the completed robot. The ECA will introduce members to practical skills such as CAD, 3D printing, mechanical assembly, tool use, and robot design. Members with different levels of experience are welcome, and more experienced students will help teach and guide newer members. Through hands-on projects, members will learn how individual components work together as part of a complete robotic system. Our main aim is to prepare for the FTC competition while creating a collaborative environment where members can experiment, solve problems, learn from mistakes, and turn their ideas into a working robot.
