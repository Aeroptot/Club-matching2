## 社团介绍

The Linguistics Club is a student-led club that explores how language works and how it shapes our understanding of society. Through interactive quizzes, discussions, presentations, and activities, we explore topics including sociolinguistics, language and gender, semantics, phonetics, and language diversity. The club aims to make linguistics accessible and engaging while encouraging students to question assumptions about language and discover how it connects to everyday life.
