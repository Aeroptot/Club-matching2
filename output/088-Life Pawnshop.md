## 社团介绍

Life Pawnshop is an ECA focusing on scientific self-exploration through cognitive psychology and community building. Instead of relying on superficial and changeable personality tests such as MBTI, this club uses psychological theories, cognitive bias experiments, value-choice games and movie analysis to help students identify what combination of personality traits makes them who they are, inner goals they might not have recognized yet, and subconscious preferences. I hope to create a safe space for students to “drop the mask”, see their true selves, and learn to build a psychological defense system against external stressors. At Life Pawnshop, we will try to be honest, even if that means we are unsure, confused, and even awkward. But we will walk out braver and more confident.
