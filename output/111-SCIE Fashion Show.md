## 社团介绍

Fashion Show is a student-led ECA where members work together to organize the annual school Fashion Show. Members will be divided into different departments and take part in different parts of the preparation, such as deciding the theme, organizing auditions, publicity, model and designer management, and planning the final show. Throughout the process, members are encouraged to share their own ideas and take responsibility for different tasks. Although our final goal is to organize the Fashion Show, the ECA is also a place for students who are interested in fashion, design, media, event planning, or simply want to be part of a creative project and work with other students.
