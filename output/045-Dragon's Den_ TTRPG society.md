## 社团介绍

Dragon's Den is a platform for SCIE students interested in tabletop roleplaying games such as Dungeons and Dragons to meet new friends and form campaigns. The whole ECA is a network of creativity. Our members post and and exchange art of their characters, discuss theories about lore and worldbuilding, and unleash their inner creative spirits when they roleplay as their characters in our magical, medieval fantasy dungeon.
