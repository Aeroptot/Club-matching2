## 社团介绍

What does it mean to be free? Is success always worth pursuing? How much of our lives should be determined by grades, university offers, competition and other people's expectations? And how can we maintain a sense of balance when the world around us constantly tells us to do more, achieve more and become more? These are not merely philosophical questions. They are questions about everyday life. This ECA explores these questions through the Inner Chapters of the Zhuangzi, one of the most imaginative works of classical Chinese philosophy. Rather than studying Zhuangzi simply as an ancient Chinese thinker, students will encounter his ideas as a provocative conversation partner for the challenges of modern student life. Through stories, paradoxes, thought experiments, close reading and panel discussions, we will explore themes including freedom, perspective, comparison, success, social expectations, identity, uncertainty, change and inner balance. This ECA creates a space where students can step outside the pressure of everyday academic life, question assumptions, encounter different perspectives, and reflect on what they genuinely value. Ultimately, Zhuangzi invites us to consider a deceptively simple question: How can we live freely in a world that constantly tells us who we should be?
