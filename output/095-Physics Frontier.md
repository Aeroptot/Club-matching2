## 社团介绍

Our club is mainly lecture-based, and I, as the leader of the club, will conduct most of these lectures. These lectures aim to provide a revision and a prevision of school contents for club members, and they will cover curriculums from A level, IGCSE, AP physics up to physics competitions like BPHO and Physics Bowl. Every lecture will be around 50 minutes and there are no after school assignments, so the learning atmosphere is quite relaxing.
